This practice set contains 12 exam-focused multiple-choice questions on Structural Organisation in Animals and Plants, aligned with the NEET Biology syllabus. 1. In animals, which type of epithelial tissue is best suited for rapid diffusion of substances, such as in the alveoli of the lungs?

A. Stratified squamous epithelium
B. Simple cuboidal epithelium
C. Simple squamous epithelium
D. Pseudostratified columnar epithelium
Answer: C

Simple squamous epithelium consists of a single layer of flat cells, which minimizes the diffusion distance and is ideal for rapid exchange of gases and nutrients, such as in the alveoli of the lungs.

2. In animals, which connective tissue is responsible for storing fat and providing insulation?

A. Areolar tissue
B. Adipose tissue
C. Dense regular connective tissue
D. Blood
Answer: B

Adipose tissue is a type of loose connective tissue that stores fat in adipocytes, providing insulation, energy storage, and cushioning for organs.

3. Which of the following animal tissues exhibits the property of excitability and conductivity?

A. Epithelial tissue
B. Connective tissue
C. Muscle tissue
D. Nervous tissue
Answer: D

Nervous tissue is specialized for excitability (responding to stimuli) and conductivity (transmitting electrical impulses), making it essential for communication and coordination in animals.

4. In dicot roots, the xylem is typically described as:

A. Exarch and polyarch
B. Endarch and polyarch
C. Exarch and tetrarch
D. Endarch and tetrarch
Answer: C

In dicot roots, the xylem is exarch (protoxylem toward the periphery) and typically tetrarch (four strands of xylem), though the number can vary.

5. Which of the following is a function of the epidermis in plants?

A. Photosynthesis
B. Storage of starch
C. Protection against water loss
D. Transport of water
Answer: C

The epidermis in plants is a protective outer layer that helps reduce water loss through the cuticle, prevents mechanical injury, and provides defense against pathogens. Photosynthesis and storage are not primary functions of the epidermis.

6. The phloem in plants is primarily responsible for the transport of:

A. Water and minerals from roots to leaves
B. Oxygen from leaves to other parts
C. Organic nutrients from leaves to other parts
D. Carbon dioxide from the atmosphere to leaves
Answer: C

Phloem is the vascular tissue in plants responsible for the translocation of organic nutrients, particularly sucrose, from photosynthetic sites (leaves) to other parts of the plant.

7. Which of the following tissues in plants is responsible for the conduction of water and minerals from the roots to the aerial parts?

A. Phloem
B. Xylem
C. Collenchyma
D. Parenchyma
Answer: B

Xylem is the vascular tissue in plants responsible for the conduction of water and dissolved minerals from the roots to the aerial parts of the plant. It consists of tracheids, vessels, xylem fibers, and xylem parenchyma. Phloem, on the other hand, conducts food (sucrose) from leaves to other parts of the plant.

8. In animals, which type of epithelial tissue is found lining the stomach and intestines and is involved in secretion and absorption?

A. Stratified squamous epithelium
B. Simple squamous epithelium
C. Cuboidal epithelium
D. Columnar epithelium
Answer: D

Columnar epithelium is found lining the stomach and intestines. It consists of tall, pillar-like cells that facilitate secretion and absorption. The tall structure provides a large surface area for these functions.

9. The Casparian strip in plant roots is a band of suberin found in the cell walls of which layer?

A. Epidermis
B. Cortex
C. Endodermis
D. Pericycle
Answer: C

The Casparian strip is a band of suberin (a waxy substance) found in the radial and transverse walls of the endodermal cells in plant roots. It acts as a barrier to regulate the flow of water and dissolved minerals into the vascular cylinder (stele).

10. Which of the following is NOT a characteristic feature of skeletal muscle fibers?

A. Multinucleated
B. Striated appearance
C. Involuntary control
D. Long and cylindrical shape
Answer: C

Skeletal muscle fibers are multinucleated, striated, and have a long, cylindrical shape. However, they are under voluntary control, unlike smooth or cardiac muscles, which are involuntary.

11. In dicot stems, the vascular bundles are typically arranged in a:

A. Scattered pattern
B. Ring pattern
C. Central core pattern
D. Random pattern
Answer: B

In dicot stems, the vascular bundles are arranged in a ring pattern. This arrangement provides structural support and facilitates the transport of water, nutrients, and food. In contrast, monocot stems have scattered vascular bundles.

12. The tissue in plants that provides mechanical support to growing parts such as young stems and leaf petioles is:

A. Sclerenchyma
B. Collenchyma
C. Parenchyma
D. Xylem
Answer: B

Collenchyma is a simple permanent tissue in plants that provides mechanical support to growing parts such as young stems, leaf petioles, and veins. It has unevenly thickened cell walls and is flexible, allowing for growth.

13. In animals, which type of connective tissue contains fat-storing cells called adipocytes?

A. Areolar tissue
B. Adipose tissue
C. Dense regular connective tissue
D. Cartilage
Answer: B

Adipose tissue is a type of loose connective tissue that contains fat-storing cells called adipocytes. It serves as an energy reserve, provides insulation, and cushions organs.

14. The Haversian system (osteon) is a structural unit of which type of animal tissue?

A. Blood
B. Bone
C. Cartilage
D. Muscle
Answer: B

The Haversian system, or osteon, is the structural unit of compact bone tissue in animals. It consists of concentric layers of bone matrix (lamellae) surrounding a central Haversian canal, which contains blood vessels and nerves.

15. In animals, which type of muscle tissue is found in the walls of hollow organs such as the stomach and blood vessels?

A. Skeletal muscle
B. Cardiac muscle
C. Smooth muscle
D. Striated muscle
Answer: C

Smooth muscle tissue is found in the walls of hollow organs such as the stomach, intestines, blood vessels, and urinary bladder. It is involuntary and lacks striations, unlike skeletal and cardiac muscles.
