PYQ-STYLE PRACTICE — NEET PYQ-STYLE PRACTICE: Cell Structure and Function (Part 1 of 2)

NEETEnglishPYQ-Style Practice

This section contains 12 exam-focused multiple-choice questions (MCQs) based on the NEET Biology syllabus topic 'Cell Structure and Function.' Each question carries +4 marks for a correct answer and -1 mark for an incorrect answer. Select the most appropriate option for each question.

1. Which of the following organelles is responsible for the synthesis of lipids and steroids in eukaryotic cells?

A. Rough endoplasmic reticulum
B. Smooth endoplasmic reticulum
C. Golgi apparatus
D. Lysosome
Answer: B

The smooth endoplasmic reticulum (SER) lacks ribosomes and is primarily involved in the synthesis of lipids, including phospholipids and steroids. It also plays a role in detoxification and calcium storage. The rough endoplasmic reticulum (RER) is associated with protein synthesis due to the presence of ribosomes.

2. The fluid mosaic model of the plasma membrane was proposed by which of the following scientists?

A. Robert Hooke and Anton van Leeuwenhoek
B. Singer and Nicolson
C. Camillo Golgi and Santiago Ramón y Cajal
D. Matthias Schleiden and Theodor Schwann
Answer: B

The fluid mosaic model, which describes the structure of the plasma membrane as a mosaic of components including phospholipids, cholesterol, proteins, and carbohydrates, was proposed by S.J. Singer and G.L. Nicolson in 1972.

3. Which of the following statements about mitochondria is INCORRECT?

A. Mitochondria are double-membraned organelles with their own DNA.
B. The inner mitochondrial membrane is folded into cristae to increase surface area.
C. Mitochondria are the site of anaerobic respiration in eukaryotic cells.
D. Mitochondria play a key role in apoptosis (programmed cell death).
Answer: C

Mitochondria are the site of aerobic respiration, not anaerobic respiration. Anaerobic respiration occurs in the cytoplasm. The other statements are correct: mitochondria have a double membrane, their own DNA, cristae for increased surface area, and are involved in apoptosis.

4. In plant cells, the cell wall is primarily composed of which of the following polysaccharides?

A. Starch
B. Glycogen
C. Cellulose
D. Chitin
Answer: C

The primary component of the plant cell wall is cellulose, a polysaccharide made up of glucose units. Starch is a storage polysaccharide in plants, glycogen is the storage polysaccharide in animals, and chitin is found in the exoskeletons of arthropods and fungal cell walls.

5. Which of the following structures is NOT found in prokaryotic cells?

A. Plasma membrane
B. Ribosomes
C. Nucleus
D. Cell wall
Answer: C

Prokaryotic cells lack a true nucleus and other membrane-bound organelles. They do possess a plasma membrane, ribosomes (70S type), and a cell wall (composed of peptidoglycan in bacteria).

6. The process by which cells engulf large particles or entire cells is known as:

A. Pinocytosis
B. Exocytosis
C. Phagocytosis
D. Osmosis
Answer: C

Phagocytosis is the process by which cells engulf large particles or entire cells, such as bacteria or debris. Pinocytosis involves the ingestion of liquid or small particles, exocytosis is the process of expelling materials from the cell, and osmosis is the movement of water across a semi-permeable membrane.

7. Which of the following is a function of the Golgi apparatus?

A. Synthesis of proteins
B. Modification, sorting, and packaging of proteins and lipids
C. Production of ATP
D. Digestion of cellular waste
Answer: B

The Golgi apparatus is involved in the modification, sorting, and packaging of proteins and lipids for secretion or delivery to other organelles. Protein synthesis occurs in ribosomes, ATP production occurs in mitochondria, and digestion of cellular waste is carried out by lysosomes.

8. The nucleolus is primarily involved in the synthesis of:

A. mRNA
B. tRNA
C. rRNA
D. DNA
Answer: C

The nucleolus is the site of ribosomal RNA (rRNA) synthesis and ribosome assembly. mRNA and tRNA are synthesized in the nucleus but not specifically in the nucleolus, and DNA replication occurs in the nucleus during the S phase of the cell cycle.

9. Which of the following statements about lysosomes is correct?

A. Lysosomes are involved in the synthesis of proteins.
B. Lysosomes contain enzymes that break down biological polymers.
C. Lysosomes are found only in plant cells.
D. Lysosomes are the site of photosynthesis.
Answer: B

Lysosomes contain hydrolytic enzymes that break down biological polymers such as proteins, lipids, carbohydrates, and nucleic acids. They are involved in digestion and waste removal and are found in animal cells, not plant cells. Protein synthesis occurs in ribosomes, and photosynthesis occurs in chloroplasts.

10. The movement of water across a selectively permeable membrane from an area of high water concentration to an area of low water concentration is called:

A. Active transport
B. Diffusion
C. Osmosis
D. Facilitated diffusion
Answer: C

Osmosis is the movement of water across a selectively permeable membrane from a region of high water concentration (low solute concentration) to a region of low water concentration (high solute concentration). Active transport requires energy, diffusion is the movement of solutes, and facilitated diffusion involves the movement of solutes via transport proteins.

11. Which of the following organelles is involved in the detoxification of drugs and poisons in liver cells?

A. Mitochondria
B. Peroxisomes
C. Lysosomes
D. Ribosomes
Answer: B

Peroxisomes contain enzymes that detoxify harmful substances, including drugs and poisons, particularly in liver cells. They also break down fatty acids and produce hydrogen peroxide as a byproduct. Mitochondria are involved in energy production, lysosomes in digestion, and ribosomes in protein synthesis.

12. Assertion: The plasma membrane is selectively permeable. Reason: It allows the passage of all substances freely across it.

A. Both assertion and reason are true, and the reason is the correct explanation of the assertion.
B. Both assertion and reason are true, but the reason is not the correct explanation of the assertion.
C. The assertion is true, but the reason is false.
D. Both assertion and reason are false.
Answer: C

The assertion is correct: the plasma membrane is selectively permeable, meaning it allows only certain substances to pass through. However, the reason is false because the plasma membrane does not allow the free passage of all substances; it regulates the movement of molecules based on size, charge, and solubility.

13. Which of the following statements regarding the endoplasmic reticulum (ER) is INCORRECT?

A. Rough ER is involved in the synthesis of secretory proteins and membrane proteins.
B. Smooth ER is the primary site for lipid synthesis and detoxification of drugs and poisons.
C. The ER membrane is continuous with the outer nuclear membrane, forming a single membrane system.
D. Both rough and smooth ER are equally abundant in all eukaryotic cells, regardless of their function.
Answer: D

The abundance of rough and smooth ER varies depending on the cell type and its function. For example, cells involved in protein secretion (e.g., pancreatic cells) have more rough ER, while cells involved in lipid metabolism or detoxification (e.g., liver cells) have more smooth ER. The other statements are correct descriptions of ER functions and structure.

14. A student observes a cell under a microscope and notes the presence of a large number of mitochondria. Which of the following cell types is the student most likely observing?

A. Adipose tissue cell
B. Red blood cell
C. Cardiac muscle cell
D. Epidermal cell of the skin
Answer: C

Cardiac muscle cells have a high energy demand due to their continuous contraction and relaxation, requiring a large number of mitochondria to produce ATP. Adipose tissue cells store fat and have fewer mitochondria, red blood cells lack mitochondria entirely, and epidermal cells have moderate mitochondrial content.

15. The fluid mosaic model of the plasma membrane proposes that:

A. The membrane is a rigid, static structure composed of phospholipids and proteins.
B. Proteins are embedded in a bilayer of phospholipids, which can move laterally within the membrane.
C. The membrane is primarily composed of carbohydrates with embedded lipids.
D. The phospholipid bilayer is impermeable to all substances, requiring active transport for everything.
Answer: B

The fluid mosaic model describes the plasma membrane as a dynamic structure where proteins are embedded in a fluid phospholipid bilayer. The phospholipids and proteins can move laterally, giving the membrane its fluidity. The membrane is selectively permeable, not impermeable.

16. Which organelle is responsible for the modification, sorting, and packaging of proteins and lipids for secretion or delivery to other organelles?

A. Endoplasmic reticulum
B. Golgi apparatus
C. Lysosome
D. Peroxisome
Answer: B

The Golgi apparatus modifies, sorts, and packages proteins and lipids received from the endoplasmic reticulum. It directs these molecules to their final destinations, such as secretion outside the cell or delivery to other organelles like lysosomes.

17. In a plant cell, the central vacuole performs all of the following functions EXCEPT:

A. Storage of pigments, toxins, and waste products
B. Maintenance of turgor pressure to provide structural support
C. Synthesis of ATP through oxidative phosphorylation
D. Breakdown of macromolecules using hydrolytic enzymes
Answer: C

The central vacuole in plant cells stores pigments, toxins, and waste products, maintains turgor pressure, and can break down macromolecules. However, ATP synthesis via oxidative phosphorylation occurs in mitochondria, not the vacuole.

18. Which of the following is a characteristic feature of prokaryotic cells but NOT eukaryotic cells?

A. Presence of a nucleus enclosed by a nuclear membrane
B. Circular DNA not associated with histone proteins
C. Membrane-bound organelles like mitochondria and Golgi apparatus
D. Presence of ribosomes for protein synthesis
Answer: B

Prokaryotic cells lack a nucleus and membrane-bound organelles. Their DNA is circular and not associated with histone proteins, unlike eukaryotic cells, which have linear DNA complexed with histones. Both prokaryotes and eukaryotes have ribosomes.

19. A mutation disrupts the function of the signal recognition particle (SRP) in a cell. Which of the following processes would be most directly affected?

A. DNA replication in the nucleus
B. Targeting of secretory proteins to the endoplasmic reticulum
C. Synthesis of lipids in the smooth ER
D. Degradation of damaged proteins in the cytoplasm
Answer: B

The signal recognition particle (SRP) is responsible for targeting secretory and membrane proteins to the rough endoplasmic reticulum (ER) during translation. A disruption in SRP function would impair this process, preventing these proteins from being properly synthesized and folded in the ER.

20. The cytoskeleton of a eukaryotic cell is composed of all of the following EXCEPT:

A. Microtubules
B. Microfilaments
C. Intermediate filaments
D. Phospholipids
Answer: D

The cytoskeleton is composed of microtubules, microfilaments (actin filaments), and intermediate filaments. Phospholipids are components of the plasma membrane and organelle membranes, not the cytoskeleton.

21. Which of the following is a function of the nucleolus?

A. Storage of genetic material (DNA)
B. Synthesis of ribosomal RNA (rRNA) and assembly of ribosomes
C. Production of ATP through cellular respiration
D. Modification and sorting of proteins
Answer: B

The nucleolus is the site of ribosomal RNA (rRNA) synthesis and the assembly of ribosomal subunits. It does not store DNA (which is in the nucleus), produce ATP (mitochondria), or modify proteins (Golgi apparatus).

22. A cell is observed to have a cell wall, chloroplasts, and a large central vacuole. This cell is most likely derived from:

A. An animal
B. A bacterium
C. A plant
D. A fungus
Answer: C

The presence of a cell wall, chloroplasts, and a large central vacuole are characteristic features of plant cells. Animal cells lack cell walls and chloroplasts, bacteria lack chloroplasts and a large central vacuole, and fungi have cell walls but lack chloroplasts.

23. Which of the following processes occurs in the peroxisomes of eukaryotic cells?

A. Breakdown of glucose to produce ATP
B. Detoxification of hydrogen peroxide (H2O2) and oxidation of fatty acids
C. Synthesis of proteins destined for secretion
D. Storage of calcium ions for cellular signaling
Answer: B

Peroxisomes contain enzymes like catalase that detoxify hydrogen peroxide and oxidize fatty acids. They are not involved in ATP production (mitochondria), protein synthesis (ribosomes/rough ER), or calcium storage (smooth ER).
