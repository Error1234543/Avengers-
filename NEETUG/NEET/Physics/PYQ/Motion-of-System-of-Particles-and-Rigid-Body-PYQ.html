This section contains 12 multiple-choice questions (MCQs) based on the NEET exam pattern for the topic 'Motion of System of Particles and Rigid Body'. Each question carries 4 marks, with a negative marking of -1 for incorrect answers. Choose the correct option for each question.

1. A uniform rod of length L and mass M is pivoted at one end and released from a horizontal position. The angular speed of the rod when it becomes vertical is:

A. √(3g/L)
B. √(2g/L)
C. √(g/L)
D. √(6g/L)
Answer: A

Using the principle of conservation of energy, the potential energy lost by the rod when it moves from horizontal to vertical position is converted into rotational kinetic energy. The moment of inertia of a rod pivoted at one end is (1/3)ML². Equating the potential energy (MgL/2) to the rotational kinetic energy (1/2 * I * ω²), we get ω = √(3g/L).

2. The moment of inertia of a thin uniform rod of mass M and length L about an axis perpendicular to the rod and passing through its center is I. The moment of inertia about a parallel axis at one end of the rod is:

A. I/2
B. 2I
C. 3I
D. 4I/3
Answer: D

Using the parallel axis theorem, the moment of inertia about a parallel axis at a distance d from the center is I + Md². Here, d = L/2, and I = (1/12)ML². Thus, the moment of inertia about the end is (1/12)ML² + M(L/2)² = (1/3)ML² = 4I/3.

3. A solid sphere of mass M and radius R rolls down an inclined plane without slipping. The acceleration of its center of mass is:

A. (5/7)g sinθ
B. (2/5)g sinθ
C. (3/5)g sinθ
D. (5/9)g sinθ
Answer: A

For a rolling sphere, the acceleration of the center of mass is given by a = g sinθ / (1 + I/MR²), where I is the moment of inertia of the sphere about its center (I = (2/5)MR²). Substituting, we get a = (5/7)g sinθ.

4. Two particles of masses m₁ and m₂ are connected by a light rod of length L. The moment of inertia of the system about an axis perpendicular to the rod and passing through the center of mass is:

A. (m₁m₂L²)/(m₁ + m₂)
B. (m₁m₂L²)/(m₁ - m₂)
C. (m₁ + m₂)L²/4
D. (m₁m₂L²)/4(m₁ + m₂)
Answer: A

The center of mass divides the rod in the ratio m₂:m₁. The distances of m₁ and m₂ from the center of mass are (m₂L)/(m₁ + m₂) and (m₁L)/(m₁ + m₂), respectively. The moment of inertia is m₁[(m₂L)/(m₁ + m₂)]² + m₂[(m₁L)/(m₁ + m₂)]² = (m₁m₂L²)/(m₁ + m₂).

5. A disc of mass M and radius R is rotating with angular velocity ω about an axis perpendicular to its plane and passing through its center. The kinetic energy of the disc is:

A. (1/2)MR²ω²
B. (1/4)MR²ω²
C. MR²ω²
D. (1/8)MR²ω²
Answer: B

The moment of inertia of a disc about its center is (1/2)MR². The rotational kinetic energy is (1/2)Iω² = (1/2)(1/2)MR²ω² = (1/4)MR²ω².

6. The angular momentum of a particle of mass m moving with velocity v in a circular path of radius r is:

A. mvr
B. mv²r
C. mvr²
D. mv/r
Answer: A

Angular momentum (L) is given by L = r × p, where p is the linear momentum (mv). For circular motion, the magnitude of angular momentum is L = mvr.

7. A rigid body is rotating about a fixed axis with angular velocity ω. If the angular momentum of the body is L, the moment of inertia of the body about the axis is:

A. L/ω
B. Lω
C. ω/L
D. L²/ω
Answer: A

Angular momentum (L) is related to moment of inertia (I) and angular velocity (ω) by the equation L = Iω. Thus, I = L/ω.

8. A thin uniform ring of mass M and radius R is rotating about an axis perpendicular to its plane and passing through its center. If the angular velocity is ω, the kinetic energy of the ring is:

A. (1/2)MR²ω²
B. MR²ω²
C. (1/4)MR²ω²
D. 2MR²ω²
Answer: A

The moment of inertia of a ring about its center is MR². The rotational kinetic energy is (1/2)Iω² = (1/2)MR²ω².

9. The torque required to stop a rotating wheel of moment of inertia I and angular velocity ω in time t is:

A. Iω/t
B. Iω²/t
C. I/tω
D. Iωt
Answer: A

Torque (τ) is the rate of change of angular momentum. The change in angular momentum is Iω, and the time taken is t. Thus, τ = Iω/t.

10. A uniform rod of length L and mass M is free to rotate about a horizontal axis passing through one end. The minimum angular velocity required to rotate the rod to a vertical position is:

A. √(3g/L)
B. √(2g/L)
C. √(g/L)
D. √(6g/L)
Answer: A

Using conservation of energy, the initial kinetic energy (1/2)Iω² must equal the potential energy gained (MgL/2). The moment of inertia I = (1/3)ML². Solving, ω = √(3g/L).

11. The radius of gyration of a body about an axis is defined as the distance from the axis at which:

A. the entire mass of the body can be considered to be concentrated for calculating moment of inertia
B. the moment of inertia is zero
C. the angular momentum is maximum
D. the torque is minimum
Answer: A

The radius of gyration (k) is the distance from the axis of rotation where the entire mass of the body can be assumed to be concentrated to give the same moment of inertia. It is defined by I = Mk².

12. A solid cylinder of mass M and radius R rolls down an inclined plane without slipping. The ratio of its translational kinetic energy to rotational kinetic energy is:

A. 1:1
B. 2:1
C. 3:1
D. 1:2
Answer: B

For a rolling cylinder, the total kinetic energy is the sum of translational (1/2 Mv²) and rotational (1/2 Iω²) kinetic energies. The moment of inertia I = (1/2)MR², and ω = v/R. Thus, rotational kinetic energy = (1/4)Mv². The ratio of translational to rotational kinetic energy is (1/2 Mv²) : (1/4 Mv²) = 2:1.

13. A disc of radius R and mass M is rolling without slipping on a horizontal surface with velocity v. The ratio of its rotational kinetic energy to translational kinetic energy is:

A. 1:1
B. 1:2
C. 2:1
D. 1:3
Answer: B

Rotational KE = ½Iω² = ½(MR²/2)(v/R)² = Mv²/4. Translational KE = ½Mv². The ratio is (Mv²/4) : (½Mv²) = 1:2.

14. The angular momentum of a particle of mass m moving with velocity v in a circular path of radius r is L. If the radius is doubled and the velocity halved, the new angular momentum is:

A. L/2
B. L
C. 2L
D. L/4
Answer: B

Angular momentum L = mvr. New radius = 2r, new velocity = v/2. New L = m(v/2)(2r) = mvr = L. Angular momentum remains unchanged.

15. A solid sphere of mass M and radius R rolls down an inclined plane of height h. The velocity of its center of mass at the bottom is:

A. √(10gh/7)
B. √(2gh)
C. √(4gh/3)
D. √(gh)
Answer: A

Using energy conservation: Mgh = ½Mv² + ½Iω². For a solid sphere, I = (2/5)MR² and ω = v/R. Solving gives v = √(10gh/7).

16. A thin uniform rod of length L and mass M is rotating about an axis perpendicular to its length and passing through one end. If the angular velocity is ω, the kinetic energy of the rod is:

A. (1/6)ML²ω²
B. (1/2)ML²ω²
C. (1/3)ML²ω²
D. (1/4)ML²ω²
Answer: A

Moment of inertia I = ML²/3. Kinetic energy = ½Iω² = ½(ML²/3)ω² = (1/6)ML²ω².

17. Two point masses, each of mass m, are placed at the ends of a massless rod of length L. The radius of gyration of the system about an axis perpendicular to the rod and passing through its center is:

A. L/√2
B. L/2
C. L/√8
D. L
Answer: B

Moment of inertia I = 2m(L/2)² = mL²/2. Radius of gyration k = √(I/M), where M = 2m. Thus, k = √(mL²/2 / 2m) = L/2.

18. A cylinder of mass M and radius R is rolling without slipping on a horizontal surface. If the velocity of its center of mass is v, the total kinetic energy of the cylinder is:

A. (3/4)Mv²
B. (1/2)Mv²
C. Mv²
D. (1/4)Mv²
Answer: A

Total KE = Translational KE + Rotational KE = ½Mv² + ½Iω². For a cylinder, I = MR²/2 and ω = v/R. Thus, total KE = ½Mv² + ½(MR²/2)(v/R)² = (3/4)Mv².

19. The moment of inertia of a uniform circular disc of radius R and mass M about an axis perpendicular to its plane and passing through its edge is:

A. (3/2)MR²
B. MR²
C. (1/2)MR²
D. (5/4)MR²
Answer: A

Using the parallel axis theorem: I = I_cm + MR², where I_cm = MR²/2. Thus, I = MR²/2 + MR² = (3/2)MR².

20. A particle of mass m is moving in a circular path of radius r with angular velocity ω. The centripetal force acting on the particle is:

A. mω²r
B. mωr
C. mωr²
D. mω²r²
Answer: A

Centripetal force F = mv²/r = m(ωr)²/r = mω²r.

21. A rigid body is rotating about a fixed axis with angular acceleration α. If the torque acting on the body is τ and its moment of inertia is I, the relationship between them is:

A. τ = Iα
B. τ = I/α
C. τ = Iα²
D. τ = I²α
Answer: A

The torque τ is related to angular acceleration α by the equation τ = Iα, where I is the moment of inertia of the body about the axis of rotation.

22. A thin uniform rod of length L and mass M is pivoted at one end and released from rest in the horizontal position. The angular acceleration of the rod just after release is: (Moment of inertia of a rod about an axis through one end perpendicular to its length is ML²/3)

A. 3g/(2L)
B. 2g/(3L)
C. g/(2L)
D. g/L
Answer: A

When the rod is released from the horizontal position, the torque due to gravity about the pivot is τ = Mg(L/2). The moment of inertia of the rod about the pivot is I = ML²/3. Using τ = Iα, we get Mg(L/2) = (ML²/3)α. Solving for α, the angular acceleration, gives α = 3g/(2L).

23. Two particles of masses m₁ and m₂ are connected by a light rigid rod of length r. The system rotates about an axis perpendicular to the rod and passing through the center of mass of the system. The moment of inertia of the system about this axis is:

A. (m₁m₂/(m₁ + m₂))r²
B. (m₁ + m₂)r²
C. (m₁m₂)r²
D. (m₁ + m₂)r²/4
Answer: A

The moment of inertia of a system of two particles about an axis passing through their center of mass and perpendicular to the rod is given by the reduced mass formula. The distance of each particle from the center of mass is (m₂r)/(m₁ + m₂) for m₁ and (m₁r)/(m₁ + m₂) for m₂. The moment of inertia is m₁[(m₂r)/(m₁ + m₂)]² + m₂[(m₁r)/(m₁ + m₂)]², which simplifies to (m₁m₂/(m₁ + m₂))r².
