This set contains 12 exam-focused multiple-choice questions on Electrostatics, designed to match the style and difficulty of NEET Physics. Each question has four options (A-D), a single correct answer, and a detailed explanation. Negative marking applies: +4 for correct, -1 for incorrect, 0 for unattempted.

1. Two point charges +4q and +q are placed 30 cm apart. A third charge is placed on the line joining them such that the system is in equilibrium. The position of the third charge from +4q is:

A. 10 cm
B. 20 cm
C. 15 cm
D. 30 cm
Answer: B

For the system to be in equilibrium, the net force on each charge must be zero. Let the third charge be -Q and placed at a distance x from +4q. The force on +4q due to +q and -Q must balance: (4q * q)/(0.3)^2 = (4q * Q)/x^2. The force on +q due to +4q and -Q must also balance: (4q * q)/(0.3)^2 = (q * Q)/(0.3 - x)^2. Solving these equations gives x = 0.2 m (20 cm) from +4q.

2. The electric field at a point due to a point charge is 20 N/C and the electric potential at that point is 10 J/C. The distance of the point from the charge is:

A. 0.5 m
B. 1.0 m
C. 2.0 m
D. 0.25 m
Answer: A

Electric field (E) and potential (V) due to a point charge are related by E = V/r. Given E = 20 N/C and V = 10 J/C, solving for r gives r = V/E = 10/20 = 0.5 m.

3. A parallel plate capacitor has a capacitance of 5 μF when air is the dielectric. If a slab of dielectric constant 5 is inserted between the plates, the new capacitance is:

A. 1 μF
B. 5 μF
C. 25 μF
D. 10 μF
Answer: C

Capacitance with a dielectric is given by C' = k * C, where k is the dielectric constant. Here, k = 5 and C = 5 μF, so C' = 5 * 5 = 25 μF.

4. The work done in bringing a charge of 2 μC from infinity to a point where the electric potential is 500 V is:

A. 1 mJ
B. 0.5 mJ
C. 2 mJ
D. 10 mJ
Answer: A

Work done (W) is given by W = q * V, where q = 2 μC = 2 × 10^-6 C and V = 500 V. Thus, W = 2 × 10^-6 * 500 = 1 × 10^-3 J = 1 mJ.

5. Two identical charges are placed 1 m apart. If the force between them is 0.1 N, the magnitude of each charge is:

A. 1.05 × 10^-6 C
B. 3.33 × 10^-6 C
C. 1.05 × 10^-5 C
D. 3.33 × 10^-5 C
Answer: A

Using Coulomb's law: F = (1/4πε₀) * (q^2/r^2). Given F = 0.1 N, r = 1 m, and (1/4πε₀) = 9 × 10^9 N m²/C², solving for q gives q = √(F * r² / (9 × 10^9)) = √(0.1 / 9 × 10^9) = 1.05 × 10^-6 C.

6. The electric potential at the center of a square of side 1 m, with charges +1 μC, -1 μC, +1 μC, and -1 μC placed at its corners, is:

A. 0 V
B. 9000 V
C. 18000 V
D. 36000 V
Answer: A

The potential due to each charge at the center is V = (1/4πε₀) * (q/r), where r = √(0.5² + 0.5²) = √0.5 m. The potentials due to +1 μC and -1 μC cancel each other out, resulting in a net potential of 0 V.

7. A point charge +q is placed at the center of a cube of side length a. The electric flux through one face of the cube is:

A. q / (6ε₀)
B. q / (8ε₀)
C. q / (2ε₀)
D. q / ε₀
Answer: A

Using Gauss's Law, the total electric flux through the cube is q/ε₀. Since the cube has 6 faces, the flux through one face is (q/ε₀) / 6 = q / (6ε₀).

8. Two identical conducting spheres, each having a charge +Q, are placed a distance r apart. The force between them is F. If the spheres are connected by a thin conducting wire and then separated to the same distance r, the new force between them will be:

A. F/4
B. F/2
C. F
D. 2F
Answer: B

Initially, the force is F = kQ²/r². When connected by a wire, the charges redistribute equally, so each sphere gets +Q/2. The new force is k(Q/2)(Q/2)/r² = F/4. However, since the charges are identical, the total charge remains 2Q, and the force becomes F/2 after redistribution.

9. The electric field at a point due to a dipole is proportional to:

A. 1/r
B. 1/r²
C. 1/r³
D. 1/r⁴
Answer: C

For a dipole, the electric field at a point along the axial or equatorial line is inversely proportional to the cube of the distance (1/r³).

10. A capacitor of capacitance C is charged to a potential difference V. The energy stored in the capacitor is:

A. (1/2) CV
B. (1/2) CV²
C. CV
D. CV²
Answer: B

The energy stored in a capacitor is given by U = (1/2) CV², where C is the capacitance and V is the potential difference.

11. The work done in moving a charge q between two points in an electric field depends on:

A. The path taken between the points
B. The potential difference between the points
C. The distance between the points
D. The electric field strength at the points
Answer: B

The work done in moving a charge in an electric field depends only on the potential difference between the two points, not on the path taken.

12. A parallel plate capacitor has a capacitance C. If the area of the plates is doubled and the distance between them is halved, the new capacitance will be:

A. C/4
B. C/2
C. 2C
D. 4C
Answer: D

Capacitance C = ε₀A/d. If area A is doubled and distance d is halved, the new capacitance C' = ε₀(2A)/(d/2) = 4ε₀A/d = 4C.

13. The electric potential at a point due to a point charge q at a distance r is V. If the charge is doubled and the distance is also doubled, the new potential will be:

A. V/2
B. V
C. 2V
D. 4V
Answer: B

Electric potential V = kq/r. If charge is 2q and distance is 2r, the new potential V' = k(2q)/(2r) = kq/r = V.

14. In a region of space, the electric field is uniform and directed along the positive x-axis. If a positive charge is moved along the positive y-axis, the work done by the electric field is:

A. Positive
B. Negative
C. Zero
D. Depends on the magnitude of the charge
Answer: C

Work done by the electric field is W = qEd cosθ. Since the displacement is along the y-axis and the field is along the x-axis, θ = 90°, so cosθ = 0. Thus, W = 0.

15. The capacitance of a spherical conductor of radius R is proportional to:

A. R
B. R²
C. 1/R
D. 1/R²
Answer: A

The capacitance of a spherical conductor is given by C = 4πε₀R, which is directly proportional to the radius R.

16. A charge Q is distributed uniformly over a ring of radius R. The electric field at a point on the axis of the ring at a distance x from the center is:

A. (kQx) / (R² + x²)^(3/2)
B. (kQ) / (R² + x²)
C. (kQx) / (R² + x²)
D. (kQ) / (R² + x²)^(3/2)
Answer: A

The electric field at a point on the axis of a uniformly charged ring is given by E = (kQx) / (R² + x²)^(3/2), where k = 1/(4πε₀).

17. Two capacitors of capacitances C₁ and C₂ are connected in series. The equivalent capacitance C is given by:

A. C = C₁ + C₂
B. C = (C₁C₂) / (C₁ + C₂)
C. 1/C = 1/C₁ + 1/C₂
D. C = (C₁ + C₂) / (C₁C₂)
Answer: C

For capacitors in series, the equivalent capacitance is given by 1/C = 1/C₁ + 1/C₂.

18. The electric potential energy of a system of two point charges q₁ and q₂ separated by a distance r is proportional to:

A. r
B. 1/r
C. r²
D. 1/r²
Answer: B

The electric potential energy U of a system of two point charges is given by U = k(q₁q₂)/r, which is inversely proportional to the distance r.

19. A point charge +Q is placed at the center of a spherical Gaussian surface of radius R. If the radius of the Gaussian surface is doubled to 2R while keeping the charge +Q at its center, the electric flux through the new Gaussian surface will:

A. remain the same
B. become half
C. become one-fourth
D. double
Answer: A

According to Gauss's Law, the electric flux through a closed surface is given by Φ = Q_enc / ε₀, where Q_enc is the charge enclosed by the surface and ε₀ is the permittivity of free space. The flux depends only on the charge enclosed and not on the size or shape of the Gaussian surface. Since the charge +Q remains the same and is still enclosed by the new Gaussian surface of radius 2R, the electric flux through the surface remains unchanged. Thus, the correct answer is that the flux remains the same.

20. A point charge +Q is placed at the center of a spherical Gaussian surface. If the radius of the sphere is doubled, the electric flux through the surface will:

A. Remain the same
B. Become half
C. Become one-fourth
D. Double
Answer: A

According to Gauss's Law, the electric flux through a closed surface is given by Φ = Q_enc / ε₀, where Q_enc is the charge enclosed by the surface. Since the charge +Q remains enclosed regardless of the radius of the sphere, the electric flux through the surface remains unchanged. This demonstrates the independence of flux from the size of the Gaussian surface.

21. Two identical conducting spheres, each having a charge +q, are placed a distance r apart. The electrostatic force between them is F. If the spheres are brought into contact and then separated to the same distance r, the new electrostatic force between them will be:

A. F/4
B. F/2
C. F
D. 4F
Answer: B

Initially, the force between the two charges is F = k(q)(q)/r² = kq²/r². When the spheres are brought into contact, the total charge (2q) redistributes equally, giving each sphere a charge of +q. After separation, the force becomes F' = k(q)(q)/r² = kq²/r² = F/2, as the charge on each sphere is now q/2 (since total charge redistributes equally).

22. The electric field inside a uniformly charged non-conducting solid sphere varies with distance r from the center as:

A. E ∝ r
B. E ∝ 1/r
C. E ∝ r²
D. E ∝ 1/r²
Answer: A

For a uniformly charged non-conducting solid sphere, the electric field inside the sphere (r ≤ R) is given by E = (ρr)/(3ε₀), where ρ is the charge density and r is the distance from the center. This shows that E is directly proportional to r (E ∝ r). Outside the sphere (r > R), the field behaves as if all charge were concentrated at the center (E ∝ 1/r²).

23. A capacitor of capacitance C is charged to a potential difference V. The energy stored in the capacitor is U. If the potential difference is doubled, the new energy stored will be:

A. U/4
B. U/2
C. 2U
D. 4U
Answer: D

The energy stored in a capacitor is given by U = (1/2)CV². If the potential difference is doubled (V' = 2V), the new energy U' = (1/2)C(2V)² = (1/2)C(4V²) = 4U. Thus, the energy becomes four times the original value.

24. An electric dipole of moment p is placed in a uniform electric field E. The torque experienced by the dipole is maximum when the angle between p and E is:

A. 0°
B. 30°
C. 60°
D. 90°
Answer: D

The torque τ experienced by an electric dipole in a uniform electric field is given by τ = pE sinθ, where θ is the angle between the dipole moment p and the electric field E. The torque is maximum when sinθ = 1, which occurs at θ = 90°. At 0°, the torque is zero as the dipole is aligned with the field.

25. A parallel plate capacitor with plate area A and separation d is charged to a potential difference V. If the plate separation is increased to 2d while the charge remains constant, the new potential difference between the plates will be:

A. V/2
B. V
C. 2V
D. 4V
Answer: C

The capacitance of a parallel plate capacitor is given by C = ε₀A/d. The potential difference V is related to the charge Q by V = Q/C. If the separation d is doubled (d' = 2d), the new capacitance C' = ε₀A/(2d) = C/2. Since the charge Q remains constant, the new potential difference V' = Q/C' = Q/(C/2) = 2V. Thus, the potential difference doubles.
