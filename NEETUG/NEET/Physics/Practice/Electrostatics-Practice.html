NEET Physics Practice - Electrostatics (Part 1)

This practice set contains 12 exam-focused multiple-choice questions on Electrostatics for NEET. Each question carries +4 marks for a correct answer and -1 mark for an incorrect answer. Select the most appropriate option for each question.

1. Two point charges +4 μC and +9 μC are placed 20 cm apart in vacuum. The distance from the +4 μC charge where the net electric field is zero is:

A. 8 cm from +4 μC
B. 12 cm from +4 μC
C. 8 cm from +9 μC
D. 12 cm from +9 μC
Answer: A

Let the distance from +4 μC be x. The electric field due to +4 μC is E₁ = k * 4 * 10⁻⁶ / x², and due to +9 μC is E₂ = k * 9 * 10⁻⁶ / (0.2 - x)². For net field to be zero, E₁ = E₂. Solving 4/x² = 9/(0.2 - x)² gives x = 0.08 m or 8 cm from +4 μC.

2. A conducting sphere of radius R has a charge Q uniformly distributed on its surface. The electric potential at a point inside the sphere (r < R) is:

A. Zero
B. kQ/R
C. kQ/r
D. kQ/(R - r)
Answer: B

Inside a conducting sphere, the electric field is zero, but the electric potential remains constant and equal to the potential at the surface, which is kQ/R.

3. The work done in moving a charge of 2 μC between two points separated by a potential difference of 10 V is:

A. 5 μJ
B. 10 μJ
C. 20 μJ
D. 40 μJ
Answer: C

Work done W = q * V = 2 * 10⁻⁶ C * 10 V = 20 * 10⁻⁶ J = 20 μJ.

4. Which of the following statements about electric field lines is INCORRECT?

A. Electric field lines originate from positive charges and terminate at negative charges.
B. Electric field lines can intersect each other.
C. The density of electric field lines is proportional to the magnitude of the electric field.
D. Electric field lines do not form closed loops unless there is a changing magnetic field.
Answer: B

Electric field lines never intersect because the electric field at any point has a unique direction.

5. A parallel plate capacitor has a capacitance of 10 μF when the distance between the plates is 1 mm. If the distance is increased to 2 mm, the new capacitance will be:

A. 5 μF
B. 10 μF
C. 15 μF
D. 20 μF
Answer: A

Capacitance C is inversely proportional to the distance d between the plates (C ∝ 1/d). Doubling d halves the capacitance: 10 μF / 2 = 5 μF.

6. The electric field at a distance r from an infinitely long line charge with linear charge density λ is given by:

A. E = kλ/r
B. E = kλ/r²
C. E = 2kλ/r
D. E = λ/(2πε₀r)
Answer: D

For an infinitely long line charge, the electric field is E = λ/(2πε₀r), where λ is the linear charge density and r is the radial distance.

7. The potential energy of a system of two point charges q₁ and q₂ separated by a distance r is given by:

A. U = kq₁q₂/r
B. U = kq₁q₂/r²
C. U = k(q₁ + q₂)/r
D. U = kq₁q₂r
Answer: A

The potential energy U of a system of two point charges is given by U = kq₁q₂/r, where k = 1/(4πε₀).

8. The force between two point charges q₁ and q₂ separated by a distance r in a medium of dielectric constant K is:

A. F = kq₁q₂/(Kr)
B. F = kq₁q₂/(Kr²)
C. F = kq₁q₂K/r²
D. F = kq₁q₂/(K²r²)
Answer: B

In a medium of dielectric constant K, the force between two point charges is reduced by a factor of K: F = kq₁q₂/(Kr²).

9. An electric dipole of moment p is placed in a uniform electric field E. The torque τ experienced by the dipole is:

A. τ = pE
B. τ = p × E
C. τ = p · E
D. τ = p/E
Answer: B

The torque τ on an electric dipole of moment p in a uniform electric field E is given by the cross product τ = p × E.

10. The electric field inside a spherical shell of uniform charge density is:

A. Zero
B. Proportional to the distance from the center
C. Inversely proportional to the distance from the center
D. Constant but non-zero
Answer: A

By Gauss's law, the electric field inside a spherical shell of uniform charge density is zero because there is no enclosed charge.

11. Two point charges +q and -q are placed at a distance of 2a apart. The electric potential at a point midway between the charges is:

A. Zero
B. kq/a
C. 2kq/a
D. -2kq/a
Answer: A

The electric potential due to a point charge is given by V = kq/r. At the midpoint, the distance from each charge is 'a'. The potential due to +q is kq/a, and due to -q is -kq/a. The net potential is the algebraic sum: kq/a + (-kq/a) = 0.

12. Two identical capacitors are connected first in series and then in parallel. The ratio of the equivalent capacitance in parallel to that in series is:

A. 1:1
B. 2:1
C. 4:1
D. 1:4
Answer: C

For two identical capacitors of capacitance C, the equivalent capacitance in series is C/2, and in parallel is 2C. The ratio of parallel to series capacitance is 2C : C/2 = 4:1.

13. The electric field due to an infinitely long straight wire with linear charge density λ at a distance r from the wire is:

A. λ/(2πε₀r)
B. λ/(4πε₀r²)
C. λ/(πε₀r)
D. λ/(2πε₀r²)
Answer: A

Using Gauss's Law, the electric field due to an infinitely long straight wire with linear charge density λ at a distance r is E = λ/(2πε₀r).

14. A parallel plate capacitor with plate area A and separation d is charged to a potential difference V. If the plate separation is doubled while the charge remains constant, the new potential difference is:

A. V/2
B. V
C. 2V
D. 4V
Answer: C

The capacitance of a parallel plate capacitor is C = ε₀A/d. If the separation d is doubled, the new capacitance is C' = ε₀A/(2d) = C/2. Since the charge Q remains constant, Q = CV = C'V'. Thus, V' = 2V.

15. A point charge +Q is placed at the center of a hollow conducting spherical shell of inner radius R and outer radius 2R. The electric field at a distance 1.5R from the center is:

A. Zero
B. Q / (4πε₀ (1.5R)²) directed radially outward
C. Q / (4πε₀ (2R)²) directed radially outward
D. Q / (4πε₀ R²) directed radially inward
Answer: B

For a hollow conducting spherical shell, the electric field inside the conductor (between R and 2R) is zero due to electrostatic shielding. However, at a point outside the inner radius but inside the outer radius (1.5R), the electric field is determined by the charge enclosed within that radius. Since the point charge +Q is at the center, the electric field at 1.5R is given by Gauss's law: E = Q / (4πε₀ r²), where r = 1.5R. The field is directed radially outward because the charge is positive. Thus, the correct answer is Q / (4πε₀ (1.5R)²).

16. Two identical conducting spheres A and B carry charges +6 μC and -2 μC respectively. They are brought into contact and then separated. What is the final charge on each sphere?

A. +2 μC
B. +4 μC
C. +3 μC
D. +1 μC
Answer: A

When two identical conducting spheres are brought into contact, the total charge is redistributed equally between them. The total charge before contact is +6 μC + (-2 μC) = +4 μC. After separation, each sphere will have half of the total charge, which is +4 μC / 2 = +2 μC.

17. The electric potential at a point due to a point charge Q is given by V = kQ/r, where k is Coulomb's constant. If the potential at a distance r from a charge is V, what will be the potential at a distance 3r from the same charge?

A. V/3
B. V/9
C. 3V
D. 9V
Answer: A

The electric potential V due to a point charge is inversely proportional to the distance r from the charge (V ∝ 1/r). If the distance increases from r to 3r, the potential decreases by a factor of 3. Thus, the new potential is V/3.

18. A parallel plate capacitor has a capacitance C when the plates are separated by a distance d and the space between them is filled with air. If the distance between the plates is halved and the space is filled with a dielectric of dielectric constant K = 4, the new capacitance will be:

A. 2C
B. 4C
C. 8C
D. 16C
Answer: C

The capacitance of a parallel plate capacitor is given by C = ε₀A/d, where A is the area of the plates and d is the separation between them. When the distance is halved (d → d/2), the capacitance doubles (2C). Additionally, introducing a dielectric with dielectric constant K = 4 increases the capacitance by a factor of 4. Thus, the new capacitance is 2C × 4 = 8C.
