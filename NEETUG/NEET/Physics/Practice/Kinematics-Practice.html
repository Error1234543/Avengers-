1. A particle moves along a straight line with an acceleration given by a = 3t² - 2t + 1, where a is in m/s² and t is in seconds. If the particle starts from rest at t = 0, what is its velocity at t = 2 s?

A. 4 m/s
B. 6 m/s
C. 8 m/s
D. 10 m/s
Answer: B

Velocity is the integral of acceleration with respect to time. Given a = 3t² - 2t + 1, integrate to find v: v = ∫(3t² - 2t + 1)dt = t³ - t² + t + C. Since the particle starts from rest, v = 0 at t = 0, so C = 0. Thus, v = t³ - t² + t. At t = 2 s, v = 8 - 4 + 2 = 6 m/s.

2. The displacement of a particle moving along the x-axis is given by x = 4t³ - 3t² + 2t - 5, where x is in meters and t is in seconds. What is the acceleration of the particle at t = 1 s?

A. 12 m/s²
B. 18 m/s²
C. 20 m/s²
D. 24 m/s²
Answer: B

Acceleration is the second derivative of displacement with respect to time. First, find velocity: v = dx/dt = 12t² - 6t + 2. Then, find acceleration: a = dv/dt = 24t - 6. At t = 1 s, a = 24(1) - 6 = 18 m/s².

3. Two cars start from rest and accelerate uniformly. Car A accelerates at 2 m/s² for 5 s, while Car B accelerates at 3 m/s² for 4 s. What is the ratio of the distances covered by Car A to Car B?

A. 5:6
B. 6:5
C. 25:24
D. 24:25
Answer: C

Distance covered under uniform acceleration is given by s = ut + ½at². For Car A: s_A = 0 + ½(2)(5)² = 25 m. For Car B: s_B = 0 + ½(3)(4)² = 24 m. The ratio s_A:s_B = 25:24.

4. A particle moves with a velocity v = 2t² - 4t + 3, where v is in m/s and t is in seconds. What is the displacement of the particle between t = 0 s and t = 3 s?

A. 6 m
B. 9 m
C. 12 m
D. 15 m
Answer: B

Displacement is the integral of velocity with respect to time. Given v = 2t² - 4t + 3, integrate to find s: s = ∫(2t² - 4t + 3)dt = (2/3)t³ - 2t² + 3t + C. Since s = 0 at t = 0, C = 0. Thus, s = (2/3)t³ - 2t² + 3t. At t = 3 s, s = (2/3)(27) - 2(9) + 9 = 18 - 18 + 9 = 9 m.

5. A stone is dropped from a height of 80 m. How long does it take to reach the ground? (Take g = 10 m/s²)

A. 2 s
B. 4 s
C. 6 s
D. 8 s
Answer: B

Using the equation s = ut + ½gt², where u = 0 and s = 80 m: 80 = 0 + ½(10)t². Solving for t gives t² = 16, so t = 4 s.

6. A train accelerates uniformly from rest to a velocity of 30 m/s in 10 s. What is the distance covered by the train during this time?

A. 100
B. 150
C. 200
D. 300
Answer: B

Using the equation s = ut + ½at², where u = 0 and a = (v - u)/t = (30 - 0)/10 = 3 m/s²: s = 0 + ½(3)(10)² = 150 m.

7. A car moving at 20 m/s decelerates uniformly to rest in 5 s. What is the magnitude of its deceleration?

A. 2
B. 4
C. 5
D. 10
Answer: B

Deceleration is given by a = (v - u)/t, where v = 0, u = 20 m/s, and t = 5 s: a = (0 - 20)/5 = -4 m/s². The magnitude of deceleration is 4 m/s².

8. A particle moves along a straight line such that its velocity v (in m/s) at time t (in s) is given by v = 4t - t². What is the maximum velocity attained by the particle?

A. 2
B. 4
C. 6
D. 8
Answer: B

To find the maximum velocity, set the derivative of v with respect to t to zero: dv/dt = 4 - 2t = 0. Solving for t gives t = 2 s. Substituting t = 2 s into v = 4t - t²: v = 8 - 4 = 4 m/s.

9. A particle moves along the x-axis with its position given by x = t³ - 6t² + 9t, where x is in meters and t is in seconds. At what time does the particle momentarily come to rest?

A. 1
B. 2
C. 3
D. 4
Answer: A

The particle comes to rest when its velocity v = 0. Velocity is the derivative of position: v = dx/dt = 3t² - 12t + 9. Set v = 0: 3t² - 12t + 9 = 0. Solving the quadratic equation gives t = 1 s or t = 3 s. The particle momentarily comes to rest at t = 1 s (and again at t = 3 s).

10. A ball is thrown horizontally from a height of 20 m with a velocity of 10 m/s. How far from the point of projection does the ball hit the ground? (Take g = 10 m/s²)

A. 10
B. 14.14
C. 20
D. 28.28
Answer: C

The time taken to hit the ground is found using the vertical motion equation: s = ut + ½gt², where u = 0 and s = 20 m: 20 = 0 + ½(10)t². Solving for t gives t = 2 s. The horizontal distance is given by x = v_x × t = 10 × 2 = 20 m.

11. A particle starts from rest and moves with a constant acceleration of 2 m/s². The displacement covered in the 5th second is:

A. 9 m
B. 10 m
C. 11 m
D. 12 m
Answer: A

Displacement in the nth second = u + (a/2)(2n - 1). Here u = 0, a = 2 m/s², n = 5. Displacement = 0 + (2/2)(2×5 - 1) = 1×9 = 9 m.

12. Two stones are thrown vertically upwards from the same point with the same speed of 20 m/s but at an interval of 2 s. The height at which they meet is: (g = 10 m/s²)

A. 10 m
B. 15 m
C. 20 m
D. 25 m
Answer: B

Let t be the time after the first stone is thrown when they meet. The second stone is thrown at t = 2 s, so it has been in the air for (t - 2) s. Equate their displacements: 20t - 5t² = 20(t - 2) - 5(t - 2)². Solving gives t = 3 s. Height = 20×3 - 5×3² = 60 - 45 = 15 m.

13. A car moving at 36 km/h accelerates uniformly to 72 km/h in 5 s. The distance travelled during this time is:

A. 50 m
B. 75 m
C. 100 m
D. 150 m
Answer: B

Convert speeds to m/s: 36 km/h = 10 m/s, 72 km/h = 20 m/s. Acceleration a = (20 - 10)/5 = 2 m/s². Distance s = ut + ½at² = 10×5 + ½×2×25 = 50 + 25 = 75 m.

14. The velocity-time graph of a particle is a straight line passing through the origin. The ratio of the displacement in the first half of the total time to that in the second half is:

A. 1:1
B. 1:2
C. 1:3
D. 1:4
Answer: C

For uniform acceleration, displacement ∝ t². Let total time = 2t. Displacement in first t s = k t², in next t s = k (2t)² - k t² = 3k t². Ratio = 1:3.

15. A ball is thrown vertically upwards with a speed of 20 m/s. The time after which the ball returns to the thrower is: (g = 10 m/s²)

A. 2 s
B. 4 s
C. 6 s
D. 8 s
Answer: B

Time to reach max height = u/g = 20/10 = 2 s. Total time = 2×2 = 4 s.

16. A particle moves along a straight line such that its displacement s at time t is given by s = t³ - 6t² + 3t + 4. The velocity of the particle when its acceleration is zero is:

A. -9 m/s
B. -6 m/s
C. 3 m/s
D. 9 m/s
Answer: A

Velocity v = ds/dt = 3t² - 12t + 3. Acceleration a = dv/dt = 6t - 12. Set a = 0 ⇒ t = 2 s. Velocity at t = 2 s = 3×4 - 12×2 + 3 = 12 - 24 + 3 = -9 m/s.

17. A train accelerates from rest at a constant rate α for some time and then decelerates at a constant rate β to come to rest. If the total time taken is t, the maximum velocity attained is:

A. αβt/(α + β)
B. (α + β)t/αβ
C. αt/(α + β)
D. βt/(α + β)
Answer: A

Let t₁ = time accelerating, t₂ = time decelerating. t₁ + t₂ = t. Maximum velocity v = α t₁ = β t₂ ⇒ t₁ = v/α, t₂ = v/β. So v/α + v/β = t ⇒ v = αβt/(α + β).

18. The displacement of a particle is given by x = 2t² + 3t + 4. The average velocity over the interval t = 2 s to t = 4 s is:

A. 11 m/s
B. 13 m/s
C. 15 m/s
D. 17 m/s
Answer: C

Displacement at t = 2 s: x₂ = 2×4 + 3×2 + 4 = 8 + 6 + 4 = 18 m. At t = 4 s: x₄ = 2×16 + 3×4 + 4 = 32 + 12 + 4 = 48 m. Average velocity = (48 - 18)/(4 - 2) = 30/2 = 15 m/s. Correction: 48 - 18 = 30, 30/2 = 15 m/s. (Option C is 15 m/s, but explanation shows 13 m/s was a typo; correct answer is C.)

19. A particle moves with a velocity v = 4 + 3t m/s. The displacement covered in the first 5 seconds is:

A. 50.5
B. 57.5
C. 62.5
D. 70.0
Answer: B

Displacement = ∫v dt from 0 to 5 = ∫(4 + 3t) dt = [4t + (3/2)t²]₀⁵ = 20 + (3/2)×25 = 20 + 37.5 = 57.5 m. Correction: 20 + 37.5 = 57.5 m (Option B). Recheck: 4×5 = 20, 3/2×25 = 37.5, total 57.5 m. Answer is B.

20. A body is projected vertically upwards with a velocity of 40 m/s. The maximum height reached is: (g = 10 m/s²)

A. 40.0
B. 60.0
C. 80.0
D. 100.0
Answer: C

Maximum height H = u²/(2g) = 40²/(2×10) = 1600/20 = 80 m.

21. The position of a particle is given by x = 3t² - 2t + 5. The acceleration of the particle is:

A. 2.0
B. 3.0
C. 6.0
D. 9.0
Answer: C

Velocity v = dx/dt = 6t - 2. Acceleration a = dv/dt = 6 m/s² (constant).

22. A car moving at 20 m/s is brought to rest in 4 s by applying brakes. The distance travelled during braking is:

A. 20.0
B. 40.0
C. 60.0
D. 80.0
Answer: B

Acceleration a = (0 - 20)/4 = -5 m/s². Distance s = ut + ½at² = 20×4 + ½×(-5)×16 = 80 - 40 = 40 m.
