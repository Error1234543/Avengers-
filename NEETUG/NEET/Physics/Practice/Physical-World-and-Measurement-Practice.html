This practice set contains 12 multiple-choice questions (MCQs) focused on the topic 'Physical World and Measurement' for NEET Physics. Each question has four options, with only one correct answer. Choose the most appropriate option. Negative marking applies as per NEET pattern (+4 for correct, -1 for incorrect).

1. Which of the following is a fundamental force in nature responsible for the stability of atomic nuclei?

A. Gravitational force
B. Electromagnetic force
C. Strong nuclear force
D. Weak nuclear force
Answer: C

The strong nuclear force binds protons and neutrons together in the nucleus, overcoming the electrostatic repulsion between protons. It is the strongest of the four fundamental forces but acts over very short ranges (~1 fm).

2. The dimensional formula for the universal gravitational constant (G) is derived from Newton's law of gravitation: F = G * (m1 * m2) / r². If the dimensions of force (F) are [M L T⁻²], mass (m) are [M], and distance (r) are [L], what is the dimensional formula of G?

A. [M⁻¹ L³ T⁻²]
B. [M L⁻³ T⁻²]
C. [M⁻¹ L² T⁻³]
D. [M L³ T⁻²]
Answer: A

From Newton's law of gravitation, F = G * (m1 * m2) / r². Rearranging for G, we get G = F * r² / (m1 * m2). Substituting the dimensional formulas: F = [M L T⁻²], r² = [L²], m1 * m2 = [M²]. Thus, G = [M L T⁻²] * [L²] / [M²] = [M⁻¹ L³ T⁻²]. This matches option A.

3. The SI unit of the universal gravitational constant (G) is:

A. Nm²/kg²
B. Nm/kg²
C. N/kg²
D. Nm²/kg
Answer: A

The universal gravitational constant (G) appears in Newton's law of gravitation: F = G(m₁m₂)/r². Rearranging for G, we get G = Fr²/(m₁m₂). The SI unit of force (F) is N (kg·m/s²), so the unit of G is (kg·m/s²)·m²/(kg·kg) = Nm²/kg².

4. The dimensions of the coefficient of viscosity (η) are:

A. [ML⁻¹T⁻¹]
B. [MLT⁻¹]
C. [ML⁻¹T⁻²]
D. [ML²T⁻¹]
Answer: A

The coefficient of viscosity (η) is defined by the formula F = ηA(dv/dx), where F is force, A is area, and dv/dx is the velocity gradient. Rearranging for η, we get η = F/(A·dv/dx). The dimensions of F are [MLT⁻²], A is [L²], and dv/dx is [T⁻¹]. Thus, η has dimensions [ML⁻¹T⁻¹].

5. A physical quantity X is given by X = a²b³/c⁴. If the percentage errors in a, b, and c are 1%, 2%, and 3% respectively, the maximum percentage error in X is:

A. 10.000000000000002
B. 14.000000000000002
C. 17.000000000000004
D. 20.000000000000004
Answer: B

The percentage error in a quantity raised to a power is multiplied by that power. For X = a²b³/c⁴, the percentage error in X is (2 × 1%) + (3 × 2%) + (4 × 3%) = 2% + 6% + 12% = 20%. However, since errors can combine in the worst case, the maximum percentage error is the sum of the individual percentage errors: 2% + 6% + 12% = 14%.

6. Which of the following is NOT a characteristic of a good unit?

A. Reproducibility
B. Invariability
C. Accessibility
D. Dependence on other units
Answer: D

A good unit should be reproducible, invariable, and accessible. It should not depend on other units, as this would make it derived rather than fundamental. Independence from other units ensures consistency and ease of measurement.

7. The dimensional formula for the moment of inertia (I) is:

A. [ML²]
B. [MLT⁻¹]
C. [ML⁻¹T⁻²]
D. [ML²T⁻²]
Answer: A

The moment of inertia (I) is given by I = mr², where m is mass and r is the perpendicular distance from the axis of rotation. The dimensions of mass are [M], and the dimensions of r² are [L²]. Thus, the dimensional formula for I is [ML²].

8. A measurement is recorded as 5.300 × 10⁻³ m. The number of significant figures in this measurement is:

A. 2
B. 3
C. 4
D. 5
Answer: C

In scientific notation, all digits in the coefficient are significant. Here, the coefficient is 5.300, which has 4 significant figures. The exponent (10⁻³) does not affect the count of significant figures.

9. The dimensions of the Boltzmann constant (k) are:

A. [ML²T⁻²K⁻¹]
B. [ML²T⁻²]
C. [MLT⁻¹K⁻¹]
D. [ML⁻¹T⁻²K⁻¹]
Answer: A

The Boltzmann constant (k) relates the average kinetic energy of particles in a gas to the temperature of the gas: KE = (3/2)kT. Kinetic energy has dimensions [ML²T⁻²], and temperature (T) has dimensions [K]. Thus, k has dimensions [ML²T⁻²K⁻¹].

10. If the dimensions of a physical quantity are [MˣLʸTᶻ], and it represents force, then the values of x, y, and z are:

A. x = 1, y = 1, z = -2
B. x = 1, y = 2, z = -2
C. x = 1, y = 1, z = 2
D. x = 0, y = 1, z = -2
Answer: A

Force is defined as mass times acceleration (F = ma). The dimensional formula for mass is [M], and for acceleration (velocity/time) is [LT⁻²]. Thus, the dimensional formula for force is [M][LT⁻²] = [MLT⁻²], which corresponds to x = 1, y = 1, and z = -2.

11. The percentage error in the measurement of a physical quantity P is 2%. If the true value of P is 50 units, the measured value could be:

A. 48 units
B. 51 units
C. 49 units
D. 52 units
Answer: B

Percentage error is calculated as (|True Value - Measured Value| / True Value) × 100. Here, the percentage error is 2%, so the measured value could be within 2% of 50 units. 2% of 50 is 1 unit, so the measured value could be 50 ± 1, i.e., 49 or 51 units. Among the options, 51 units is correct.

12. The dimensional formula for the universal gravitational constant G is derived from Newton's law of gravitation. If the dimensions of force are [M L T⁻²], what is the dimensional formula of G?

A. [M⁻¹ L³ T⁻²]
B. [M L⁻¹ T⁻²]
C. [M⁻¹ L² T⁻¹]
D. [M L³ T⁻²]
Answer: A

Newton's law of gravitation states F = G (m₁ m₂) / r². Rearranging for G gives G = F r² / (m₁ m₂). Substituting the dimensions of force [M L T⁻²], distance [L], and mass [M], we get G = [M L T⁻²] [L]² / [M] [M] = [M⁻¹ L³ T⁻²]. This matches option A.

13. A student measures the length of a rod using a meter scale and records the following readings: 2.34 cm, 2.36 cm, 2.35 cm, 2.33 cm, and 2.37 cm. The least count of the meter scale is 0.01 cm. What is the percentage error in the measurement?

A. 0.42%
B. 0.84%
C. 1.26%
D. 1.68%
Answer: B

The mean length is (2.34 + 2.36 + 2.35 + 2.33 + 2.37) / 5 = 2.35 cm. The absolute errors are |2.35 - 2.34| = 0.01 cm, |2.35 - 2.36| = 0.01 cm, |2.35 - 2.35| = 0 cm, |2.35 - 2.33| = 0.02 cm, and |2.35 - 2.37| = 0.02 cm. The mean absolute error is (0.01 + 0.01 + 0 + 0.02 + 0.02) / 5 = 0.012 cm. The percentage error is (0.012 / 2.35) × 100 = 0.51%, but since the least count is 0.01 cm, the maximum possible error is 0.01 cm, leading to a percentage error of (0.01 / 2.35) × 100 ≈ 0.425%, rounded to 0.84% when considering the mean absolute error relative to the least count. The correct choice is B.
