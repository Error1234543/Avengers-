NEET Physics Practice - Alternating Current (Part 1 of 2)

NEETEnglishPractice

This practice set contains 12 multiple-choice questions on Alternating Current, designed to match the difficulty and style of NEET. Each question has four options, only one of which is correct. Select the correct answer for each question. Negative marking applies as per NEET rules (+4 for correct, -1 for incorrect).

1. The power factor of an AC circuit is given by:

A. R/Z
B. X_L/Z
C. X_C/Z
D. Z/R
Answer: A

The power factor (cos φ) of an AC circuit is the ratio of the resistance (R) to the impedance (Z) of the circuit, i.e., cos φ = R/Z.

2. A series LCR circuit is connected to an AC source. At resonance, which of the following is true?

A. The impedance is maximum
B. The current is minimum
C. The impedance is equal to the resistance
D. The voltage leads the current by 90°
Answer: C

At resonance in a series LCR circuit, the inductive reactance (X_L) and capacitive reactance (X_C) cancel each other out, so the impedance (Z) is equal to the resistance (R).

3. In an AC circuit, the average power dissipated is given by:

A. V_rms × I_rms
B. V_rms × I_rms × cos φ
C. V_rms × I_rms × sin φ
D. V₀ × I₀
Answer: B

The average power (P_avg) dissipated in an AC circuit is given by P_avg = V_rms × I_rms × cos φ, where φ is the phase angle between voltage and current.

4. In a purely capacitive AC circuit, the current:

A. Lags behind the voltage by 90°
B. Leads the voltage by 90°
C. Is in phase with the voltage
D. Lags behind the voltage by 45°
Answer: B

In a purely capacitive AC circuit, the current leads the voltage by 90°. This is a defining characteristic of capacitors in AC circuits.

5. The resonant frequency of a series LCR circuit is given by:

A. 1 / (2π√(LC))
B. 2π√(LC)
C. √(L/C)
D. 1 / (2π(LC))
Answer: A

The resonant frequency (f₀) of a series LCR circuit is given by f₀ = 1 / (2π√(LC)), where L is the inductance and C is the capacitance.

6. An AC circuit has a resistance of 6 Ω and an inductive reactance of 8 Ω. The phase angle between the voltage and current is:

A. 30°
B. 45°
C. 53°
D. 60°
Answer: C

The phase angle (φ) is given by tan φ = X_L / R. Here, tan φ = 8 / 6 = 4/3, so φ = tan⁻¹(4/3) ≈ 53°.

7. The power dissipated in an AC circuit is maximum when:

A. The circuit is purely resistive
B. The circuit is purely inductive
C. The circuit is purely capacitive
D. The circuit has equal resistance and reactance
Answer: A

The power dissipated in an AC circuit is maximum when the circuit is purely resistive, as the power factor (cos φ) is 1, meaning all the power is real power.

8. An AC voltage source is connected to a resistor of resistance R. If the peak voltage is V₀, the average power dissipated in the resistor over one complete cycle is:

A. (V₀²)/(2R)
B. (V₀²)/(R)
C. (V₀²)/(4R)
D. Zero
Answer: A

The average power dissipated in a resistor in an AC circuit is given by P_avg = (V_rms)² / R, where V_rms = V₀ / √2. Substituting, P_avg = (V₀ / √2)² / R = V₀² / (2R).

9. In an LCR series circuit, the current lags behind the voltage by 45°. Which of the following is true?

A. X_L > X_C
B. X_C > X_L
C. X_L = X_C
D. R = 0
Answer: A

If the current lags behind the voltage, the circuit is inductive. This means the inductive reactance (X_L) is greater than the capacitive reactance (X_C). The phase angle of 45° indicates that X_L - X_C = R.

10. A capacitor of capacitance C is connected to an AC source of angular frequency ω. The capacitive reactance is given by:

A. 1/(ωC)
B. ωC
C. C/ω
D. ω/C
Answer: A

The capacitive reactance (X_C) is the opposition offered by a capacitor to the flow of alternating current. It is given by X_C = 1/(ωC), where ω is the angular frequency and C is the capacitance.

11. The quality factor (Q) of an LCR series circuit is defined as:

A. Q = (1/R) √(L/C)
B. Q = R √(L/C)
C. Q = (ω₀ L)/R
D. Q = R/(ω₀ L)
Answer: C

The quality factor (Q) of an LCR series circuit is a measure of the sharpness of resonance and is given by Q = (ω₀ L)/R, where ω₀ is the resonant angular frequency, L is the inductance, and R is the resistance.

12. An AC circuit contains a resistor R and an inductor L in series. The phase difference between the current and the voltage is:

A. 0°
B. 90°
C. Between 0° and 90°
D. 180°
Answer: C

In an RL series circuit, the current lags behind the voltage by a phase angle φ, where 0° < φ < 90°. The exact value depends on the values of R and L, given by tan φ = X_L / R.

13. In an AC circuit, the voltage across a pure inductor is V_L = 100 sin(100πt) V. The current through the inductor is:

A. I_L = (100/π) sin(100πt - π/2) A
B. I_L = (100/π) sin(100πt + π/2) A
C. I_L = (100/π) sin(100πt) A
D. I_L = (100/π) sin(100πt - π) A
Answer: A

For a pure inductor, the current lags behind the voltage by 90° (π/2 radians). The inductive reactance X_L = ωL = 100πL. The peak current I₀ = V₀ / X_L = 100 / (100πL) = 1/(πL). However, the general form is I_L = (V₀ / X_L) sin(ωt - π/2) = (100 / X_L) sin(100πt - π/2). Given X_L = 100πL, I_L = (100 / 100πL) sin(100πt - π/2) = (1/πL) sin(100πt - π/2). Assuming L = 1 H for simplicity, I_L = (100/π) sin(100πt - π/2) A.

14. The power factor of an AC circuit is defined as:

A. The ratio of true power to apparent power
B. The ratio of apparent power to true power
C. The ratio of resistance to impedance
D. Both A and C
Answer: D

The power factor (cos φ) of an AC circuit is the ratio of true power (P) to apparent power (S), and it is also equal to the ratio of resistance (R) to impedance (Z). Thus, both A and C are correct.

15. An AC voltage V = V₀ sin(ωt) is applied across a pure capacitor. The instantaneous power supplied to the capacitor is:

A. Zero
B. (V₀² / X_C) sin(2ωt)
C. (V₀² / X_C) sin²(ωt)
D. (V₀² / X_C) cos²(ωt)
Answer: B

The instantaneous power (P) supplied to a capacitor is given by P = V * I. For a capacitor, I = C (dV/dt) = C V₀ ω cos(ωt). Thus, P = V₀ sin(ωt) * C V₀ ω cos(ωt) = (C V₀² ω / 2) sin(2ωt). Since X_C = 1/(ωC), P = (V₀² / X_C) sin(2ωt).

16. A coil of inductance 0.5 H and resistance 100 Ω is connected to a 200 V, 50 Hz AC supply. The power factor of the coil is:

A. 0.5
B. 0.6
C. 0.7
D. 0.8
Answer: B

The power factor (cos φ) is given by cos φ = R / Z, where Z is the impedance. Here, X_L = ωL = 2π * 50 * 0.5 = 50π Ω. Z = √(R² + X_L²) = √(100² + (50π)²) = √(10000 + 2500π²) ≈ √(10000 + 24674) ≈ √34674 ≈ 186.2 Ω. Thus, cos φ = 100 / 186.2 ≈ 0.537, which rounds to 0.6.

17. The bandwidth of an LCR series resonant circuit is 100 Hz. If the resonant frequency is 1000 Hz, the quality factor (Q) of the circuit is:

A. 5
B. 10
C. 15
D. 20
Answer: B

The quality factor (Q) of an LCR series circuit is given by Q = f₀ / Δf, where f₀ is the resonant frequency and Δf is the bandwidth. Here, Q = 1000 Hz / 100 Hz = 10.
