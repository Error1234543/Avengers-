This practice set contains 12 exam-focused multiple-choice questions on Moving Charges and Magnetism, aligned with the NEET syllabus and difficulty level. Each question carries +4 marks for a correct answer and -1 mark for an incorrect answer. Select the most appropriate option for each question.

1. A charged particle of mass m and charge q enters a uniform magnetic field B with velocity v perpendicular to the field. The radius of the circular path described by the particle is given by:

A. r = mv/qB
B. r = qB/mv
C. r = mvB/q
D. r = qv/mB
Answer: A

When a charged particle moves perpendicular to a uniform magnetic field, it experiences a centripetal force due to the magnetic Lorentz force: F = qvB. This force provides the centripetal force required for circular motion: F = mv²/r. Equating the two, qvB = mv²/r, solving for r gives r = mv/qB.

2. A current-carrying circular loop of radius R is placed in a uniform magnetic field B such that the plane of the loop is perpendicular to the field. The magnetic moment of the loop is M. The torque experienced by the loop is:

A. MB
B. MB sinθ, where θ is the angle between M and B
C. Zero
D. MB cosθ
Answer: C

The torque τ on a current loop is given by τ = M × B. When the plane of the loop is perpendicular to the magnetic field, the magnetic moment M is parallel to B. Thus, the angle θ between M and B is 0°, and τ = MB sinθ = MB sin0° = 0.

3. The magnetic field at the center of a circular loop of radius R carrying current I is B. If the radius of the loop is halved and the current is doubled, the new magnetic field at the center is:

A. B
B. 2B
C. 4B
D. B/2
Answer: C

The magnetic field at the center of a circular loop is given by B = μ₀I / 2R. If the radius is halved (R' = R/2) and the current is doubled (I' = 2I), the new magnetic field B' = μ₀(2I) / 2(R/2) = μ₀(2I) / R = 4(μ₀I / 2R) = 4B.

4. A charged particle moves in a uniform magnetic field with velocity v. The magnetic force on the particle is maximum when the angle between v and B is:

A. 0°
B. 30°
C. 60°
D. 90°
Answer: D

The magnetic force on a charged particle is given by F = qvB sinθ, where θ is the angle between the velocity v and the magnetic field B. The force is maximum when sinθ = 1, i.e., θ = 90°.

5. A cyclotron is used to accelerate protons. If the magnetic field in the cyclotron is B and the radius of the dees is R, the maximum kinetic energy of the protons is:

A. (q²B²R²) / 2m
B. (qBR) / 2m
C. (q²B²R²) / m
D. (qBR)² / 2m
Answer: A

In a cyclotron, the maximum kinetic energy of a charged particle is given by K = (q²B²R²) / 2m, where q is the charge, B is the magnetic field, R is the radius of the dees, and m is the mass of the particle.

6. A straight wire of length L carrying current I is placed in a uniform magnetic field B perpendicular to the wire. The magnetic force on the wire is:

A. ILB
B. ILB sinθ
C. Zero
D. ILB cosθ
Answer: A

The magnetic force on a current-carrying wire in a uniform magnetic field is given by F = ILB sinθ, where θ is the angle between the wire and the magnetic field. If the wire is perpendicular to the field, θ = 90°, so F = ILB sin90° = ILB.

7. The magnetic field inside a toroidal solenoid of N turns carrying current I is B. If the current is doubled and the number of turns is halved, the new magnetic field is:

A. B/2
B. B
C. 2B
D. 4B
Answer: B

The magnetic field inside a toroidal solenoid is given by B = μ₀NI / 2πr, where r is the radius of the toroid. If the current is doubled (I' = 2I) and the number of turns is halved (N' = N/2), the new magnetic field B' = μ₀(N/2)(2I) / 2πr = μ₀NI / 2πr = B.

8. A charged particle of charge q and mass m is moving with velocity v in a uniform magnetic field B. If the particle moves in a circular path of radius r, the time period of its motion is:

A. 2πm / qB
B. 2πqB / m
C. 2πr / v
D. Both A and C
Answer: D

The time period T of a charged particle moving in a circular path in a uniform magnetic field is independent of its velocity and is given by T = 2πm / qB. It can also be expressed as T = 2πr / v, since v = qBr / m. Thus, both options A and C are correct.

9. A current-carrying square loop of side a is placed in a uniform magnetic field B such that the plane of the loop makes an angle θ with the field. The magnetic flux through the loop is:

A. Ba² cosθ
B. Ba² sinθ
C. Zero
D. Ba²
Answer: A

The magnetic flux Φ through a loop is given by Φ = B · A = BA cosθ, where θ is the angle between the magnetic field B and the normal to the plane of the loop. For a square loop of side a, the area A = a², so Φ = Ba² cosθ.

10. A proton moving with a velocity of 4 × 10^6 m/s enters a uniform magnetic field of 0.5 T at an angle of 30° to the field. The magnitude of the magnetic force acting on the proton is (charge of proton = 1.6 × 10^-19 C):

A. 1.6 × 10^-13 N
B. 3.2 × 10^-13 N
C. 1.6 × 10^-14 N
D. 3.2 × 10^-14 N
Answer: A

The magnetic force on a charged particle is given by F = qvB sinθ, where q is the charge, v is the velocity, B is the magnetic field, and θ is the angle between velocity and field. Substituting the values: F = (1.6 × 10^-19 C)(4 × 10^6 m/s)(0.5 T) sin(30°) = 1.6 × 10^-13 N.

11. An electron moving perpendicular to a uniform magnetic field of 0.02 T completes a circular path of radius 0.5 mm. The speed of the electron is (mass of electron = 9.1 × 10^-31 kg, charge of electron = 1.6 × 10^-19 C):

A. 1.76 × 10^6 m/s
B. 3.52 × 10^6 m/s
C. 1.76 × 10^7 m/s
D. 3.52 × 10^7 m/s
Answer: A

For a charged particle moving perpendicular to a magnetic field, the radius of the circular path is given by r = mv / (qB). Rearranging for v: v = (qBr) / m. Substituting the values: v = (1.6 × 10^-19 C)(0.02 T)(0.5 × 10^-3 m) / (9.1 × 10^-31 kg) = 1.76 × 10^6 m/s.

12. The magnetic field at a point due to a long straight wire carrying a current of 5 A is 2 × 10^-5 T. The distance of the point from the wire is:

A. 0.05 m
B. 0.1 m
C. 0.2 m
D. 0.025 m
Answer: A

The magnetic field due to a long straight wire is given by B = (μ₀ I) / (2π d). Rearranging for d: d = (μ₀ I) / (2π B). Substituting the values: d = (4π × 10^-7 T·m/A)(5 A) / (2π × 2 × 10^-5 T) = 0.05 m.

13. A charged particle moves in a circular path in a uniform magnetic field. If the radius of the path is doubled while keeping the magnetic field constant, the momentum of the particle:

A. Doubles
B. Halves
C. Remains the same
D. Becomes four times
Answer: A

The radius of the circular path is given by r = mv / (qB). If the radius is doubled, r' = 2r, then mv' / (qB) = 2mv / (qB). Thus, v' = 2v, and the momentum p = mv also doubles.

14. A rectangular loop of sides 0.1 m and 0.2 m carries a current of 2 A. The magnetic moment of the loop is:

A. 4 × 10^-2 A·m²
B. 2 × 10^-2 A·m²
C. 8 × 10^-2 A·m²
D. 1 × 10^-2 A·m²
Answer: A

The magnetic moment of a current loop is given by M = I × A, where I is the current and A is the area of the loop. Here, A = 0.1 m × 0.2 m = 0.02 m². Thus, M = (2 A)(0.02 m²) = 4 × 10^-2 A·m².
