NEET Physics Practice – Electromagnetic Induction (Part 1 of 25)

1. The self-inductance of a solenoid of length l, cross-sectional area A, and N turns is proportional to

A. N²A / l
B. NA / l
C. N²l / A
D. Nl / A
Answer: A

Self-inductance L = μ₀N²A / l, so L ∝ N²A / l.

2. A conducting rod of length l moves with velocity v perpendicular to a uniform magnetic field B. The magnitude of the induced emf between the ends of the rod is

A. Blv
B. Blv sin θ (θ = 90°)
C. Bl / v
D. Bv / l
Answer: A

Motional emf ε = Blv when v ⊥ B and l ⊥ both.

3. Two coils have mutual inductance M. If the current in the primary coil changes at a rate of 2 A/s, the magnitude of the induced emf in the secondary coil is

A. 2M
B. M / 2
C. M
D. 4M
Answer: A

ε = –M dI/dt. Here dI/dt = 2 A/s, so |ε| = 2M.

4. In an LR circuit, the current reaches 63.2% of its final value in a time equal to

A. one time constant
B. two time constants
C. half the time constant
D. the time constant squared
Answer: A

By definition, the time constant τ is the time for current to reach 1 – 1/e ≈ 63.2% of its final value.

5. The efficiency of an ideal transformer is

A. 100%
B. 50%
C. 75%
D. 90%
Answer: A

An ideal transformer has no losses, so efficiency = 100%.

6. A metal ring falls vertically into a region of uniform magnetic field directed into the page. Just as the ring enters the field, the induced current in the ring is

A. clockwise
B. counter-clockwise
C. zero
D. alternating
Answer: A

Flux into the page increases. By Lenz’s law, the induced current opposes this increase, producing its own field out of the page, hence clockwise current.

7. A 50 mH inductor carries a current that decreases uniformly from 2 A to 0 A in 0.01 s. The magnitude of the self-induced emf is

A. 10 V
B. 5 V
C. 20 V
D. 2.5 V
Answer: A

ε = –L dI/dt. dI/dt = (0 – 2 A) / 0.01 s = –200 A/s. |ε| = 50×10⁻³ H × 200 A/s = 10 V.

8. Which of the following quantities remains constant in a transformer?

A. Power (assuming ideal)
B. Voltage
C. Current
D. Resistance
Answer: A

In an ideal transformer, input power equals output power.

9. A rectangular loop of sides a and b moves with velocity v out of a uniform magnetic field B perpendicular to the plane of the loop. The induced emf in the loop is

A. zero
B. Bav
C. Bbv
D. 2B(a+b)v
Answer: A

As the loop exits the field, the flux through it remains constant (zero change), so induced emf = 0.

10. Which of the following statements is correct regarding Lenz's Law?

A. The induced current opposes the change in magnetic flux that produced it.
B. The induced emf is proportional to the rate of change of current.
C. The induced magnetic field always adds to the external magnetic field.
D. Lenz's Law is a consequence of the conservation of energy only in mechanical systems.
Answer: A

Lenz's Law states that the direction of the induced current is such that it opposes the change in magnetic flux that produced it. This is a direct consequence of the conservation of energy, ensuring that work must be done to induce the current.

11. A coil of 100 turns and area 0.01 m² is rotated from a position perpendicular to a magnetic field of 0.2 T to a position parallel to the field in 0.1 s. What is the average induced emf in the coil?

A. 0.2 V
B. 2 V
C. 20 V
D. 0.02 V
Answer: B

Initial flux Φ₁ = NBA = 100 * 0.2 * 0.01 = 0.2 Wb (perpendicular). Final flux Φ₂ = 0 Wb (parallel). Change in flux ΔΦ = Φ₂ - Φ₁ = -0.2 Wb. Time Δt = 0.1 s. Average induced emf |ε| = N|ΔΦ/Δt| = 100 * 0.2 / 0.1 = 2 V.

12. The self-inductance of a solenoid depends on:

A. The number of turns only
B. The area of cross-section and the length of the solenoid only
C. The number of turns, area of cross-section, length of the solenoid, and the permeability of the core material
D. The current flowing through the solenoid only
Answer: C

The self-inductance (L) of a solenoid is given by L = μ₀μᵣN²A/l, where μ₀ is the permeability of free space, μᵣ is the relative permeability of the core, N is the number of turns, A is the area of cross-section, and l is the length of the solenoid. Thus, it depends on all these factors.

13. In an AC generator, the induced emf is maximum when the plane of the coil is:

A. Parallel to the magnetic field
B. Perpendicular to the magnetic field
C. At 45° to the magnetic field
D. Independent of the orientation
Answer: A

The induced emf in an AC generator is given by ε = NBAω sin(ωt). It is maximum when sin(ωt) = 1, which occurs when the plane of the coil is parallel to the magnetic field (angle between the normal to the coil and the field is 90°).

14. The mutual inductance between two coils is 0.5 H. If the current in the primary coil changes from 2 A to 6 A in 0.2 s, what is the induced emf in the secondary coil?

A. 5 V
B. 10 V
C. 15 V
D. 20 V
Answer: B

The induced emf (ε) in the secondary coil is given by ε = -M(dI/dt), where M = 0.5 H, dI = 6 - 2 = 4 A, and dt = 0.2 s. Thus, |ε| = 0.5 * (4 / 0.2) = 10 V.

15. A conducting loop is pulled out of a magnetic field with a constant velocity. The induced current in the loop:

A. Increases linearly with time
B. Decreases linearly with time
C. Remains constant
D. First increases then decreases
Answer: C

When the loop is pulled out of the magnetic field with constant velocity, the rate of change of magnetic flux (dΦ/dt) remains constant. Since induced emf ε = -dΦ/dt, and current I = ε/R (R is resistance), the induced current remains constant.

16. A square loop of side 0.1 m is placed in a magnetic field of 0.2 T, perpendicular to the plane of the loop. If the loop is rotated by 180° in 0.1 s, what is the average induced emf in the loop?

A. 0.02 V
B. 0.04 V
C. 0.08 V
D. 0.16 V
Answer: B

Initial flux Φ₁ = BA = 0.2 * (0.1)² = 0.002 Wb. After 180° rotation, the flux is Φ₂ = -0.002 Wb (direction reversed). Change in flux ΔΦ = Φ₂ - Φ₁ = -0.004 Wb. Time Δt = 0.1 s. Average induced emf |ε| = |ΔΦ/Δt| = 0.004 / 0.1 = 0.04 V.
