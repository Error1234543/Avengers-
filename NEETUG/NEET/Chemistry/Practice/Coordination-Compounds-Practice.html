1. Which of the following is the correct IUPAC name for the coordination compound [Co(NH₃)₄Cl₂]Cl?

A. Tetraamminedichlorocobalt(III) chloride
B. Tetraamminedichlorocobalt(II) chloride
C. Diamminetetraamminecobalt(III) chloride
D. Tetraamminedichlorocobaltate(III) chloride
Answer: A

The complex ion is [Co(NH₃)₄Cl₂]⁺, with cobalt in the +3 oxidation state. The ligands are named in alphabetical order: ammine (NH₃) before chloro (Cl). The counter ion is chloride (Cl⁻). Thus, the correct IUPAC name is tetraamminedichlorocobalt(III) chloride.

2. The coordination number of the central metal ion in [Fe(C₂O₄)₃]³⁻ is:

A. 3
B. 4
C. 6
D. 8
Answer: C

Oxalate (C₂O₄²⁻) is a bidentate ligand, meaning each oxalate ion occupies two coordination sites. With three oxalate ligands, the total coordination number is 3 × 2 = 6.

3. The magnetic moment of [Mn(CN)₆]⁴⁻ is 1.73 BM. The oxidation state and number of unpaired electrons in Mn are:

A. +2, 1
B. +2, 5
C. +3, 2
D. +4, 3
Answer: A

The magnetic moment of 1.73 BM corresponds to 1 unpaired electron (μ = √(n(n+2)) BM). CN⁻ is a strong-field ligand, causing pairing of electrons. Mn in [Mn(CN)₆]⁴⁻ is in the +2 oxidation state (d⁵ configuration), with 1 unpaired electron in a low-spin complex.

4. Which of the following statements about the complex [Ni(CN)₄]²⁻ is correct?

A. It is paramagnetic and tetrahedral.
B. It is diamagnetic and square planar.
C. It is paramagnetic and square planar.
D. It is diamagnetic and tetrahedral.
Answer: B

CN⁻ is a strong-field ligand, causing pairing of electrons in Ni²⁺ (d⁸ configuration). The complex is square planar and diamagnetic, as all electrons are paired.

5. The primary valence (oxidation state) and secondary valence (coordination number) of cobalt in [Co(en)₂Cl₂]Cl are, respectively:

A. +3, 4
B. +3, 6
C. +2, 4
D. +2, 6
Answer: B

Ethylenediamine (en) is a bidentate ligand, and Cl⁻ is monodentate. The complex has two en ligands (4 coordination sites) and two Cl⁻ ligands (2 coordination sites), totaling 6. The oxidation state of cobalt is +3 (one Cl⁻ is outside the coordination sphere as a counter ion).

6. Which of the following ligands is ambidentate?

A. NH₃
B. NO₂⁻
C. H₂O
D. Cl⁻
Answer: B

An ambidentate ligand can bind to the central metal ion through two different donor atoms. NO₂⁻ can bind through nitrogen (nitro) or oxygen (nitrito).

7. The correct order of increasing crystal field splitting energy (Δ₀) for the complexes [Co(H₂O)₆]³⁺, [Co(NH₃)₆]³⁺, and [Co(CN)₆]³⁻ is:

A. [Co(H₂O)₆]³⁺ < [Co(NH₃)₆]³⁺ < [Co(CN)₆]³⁻
B. [Co(CN)₆]³⁻ < [Co(NH₃)₆]³⁺ < [Co(H₂O)₆]³⁺
C. [Co(NH₃)₆]³⁺ < [Co(H₂O)₆]³⁺ < [Co(CN)₆]³⁻
D. [Co(CN)₆]³⁻ < [Co(H₂O)₆]³⁺ < [Co(NH₃)₆]³⁺
Answer: A

The crystal field splitting energy (Δ₀) increases with the strength of the ligand. The spectrochemical series orders ligands as: H₂O < NH₃ < CN⁻. Thus, Δ₀ increases in the order [Co(H₂O)₆]³⁺ < [Co(NH₃)₆]³⁺ < [Co(CN)₆]³⁻.

8. In the complex [Pt(NH₃)₂Cl₂], the hybridisation of the central metal ion is:

A. sp³
B. dsp²
C. sp³d²
D. d²sp³
Answer: B

[Pt(NH₃)₂Cl₂] is a square planar complex. Pt²⁺ (d⁸) undergoes dsp² hybridisation, using one d orbital, one s orbital, and two p orbitals to form four hybrid orbitals arranged in a square plane.

9. Which of the following complexes does NOT follow the Effective Atomic Number (EAN) rule?

A. [Fe(CN)₆]⁴⁻
B. [Co(NH₃)₆]³⁺
C. [Ni(CO)₄]
D. [Cr(NH₃)₆]³⁺
Answer: D

The EAN rule states that the sum of the electrons of the metal ion and the electrons donated by the ligands should equal the atomic number of the next noble gas. For [Cr(NH₃)₆]³⁺, Cr³⁺ has 24 - 3 = 21 electrons. Each NH₃ donates 2 electrons, totaling 12. EAN = 21 + 12 = 33, which does not match the atomic number of any noble gas (Kr = 36). The other complexes satisfy the EAN rule.

10. The coordination number of cobalt in K₃[Co(C₂O₄)₃] is:

A. 3
B. 4
C. 6
D. 8
Answer: C

Oxalate (C₂O₄²⁻) is a bidentate ligand, meaning it occupies two coordination sites. With three oxalate ligands, the coordination number is 3 × 2 = 6.

11. The magnetic moment of [Fe(CN)₆]⁴⁻ is zero. This indicates that the complex is:

A. High-spin octahedral
B. Low-spin octahedral
C. Tetrahedral
D. Square planar
Answer: B

CN⁻ is a strong-field ligand that causes pairing of electrons. A magnetic moment of zero indicates no unpaired electrons, confirming a low-spin octahedral complex.

12. The crystal field stabilization energy (CFSE) for a d⁶ ion in an octahedral field is highest in:

A. Weak field ligands
B. Strong field ligands
C. Tetrahedral field
D. Square planar field
Answer: B

For a d⁶ ion, strong-field ligands cause maximum pairing of electrons, resulting in the highest CFSE (e.g., -2.4 Δ₀ for low-spin d⁶). Weak-field ligands result in lower CFSE due to unpaired electrons.

13. Which of the following complexes does NOT show geometrical isomerism?

A. [Pt(NH₃)₂Cl₂]
B. [Co(NH₃)₄Cl₂]⁺
C. [Co(NH₃)₅Cl]²⁺
D. [Ni(CN)₄]²⁻
Answer: C

Geometrical isomerism requires at least two different types of ligands in specific arrangements. [Co(NH₃)₅Cl]²⁺ has only one chloride ligand, so it cannot exhibit geometrical isomerism.

14. The correct formula for potassium hexacyanoferrate(II) is:

A. K₄[Fe(CN)₆]
B. K₃[Fe(CN)₆]
C. K₂[Fe(CN)₆]
D. K[Fe(CN)₆]
Answer: A

Hexacyanoferrate(II) has Fe in +2 oxidation state and CN⁻ as ligands (total charge -4). Potassium (K⁺) balances the charge, so the formula is K₄[Fe(CN)₆].

15. In the coordination compound [Co(NH₃)₄Cl₂]Cl, the coordination number and oxidation state of cobalt are respectively:

A. 6 and +3
B. 4 and +2
C. 6 and +2
D. 4 and +3
Answer: A

The coordination compound [Co(NH₃)₄Cl₂]Cl dissociates into [Co(NH₃)₄Cl₂]⁺ and Cl⁻. The central metal ion Co is bonded to 4 NH₃ molecules and 2 Cl⁻ ions, giving a coordination number of 6. To determine the oxidation state, let x be the oxidation state of Co. The overall charge of the complex ion [Co(NH₃)₄Cl₂]⁺ is +1. NH₃ is neutral, and each Cl⁻ has a -1 charge. Thus, x + 4(0) + 2(-1) = +1 → x - 2 = +1 → x = +3. Therefore, the coordination number is 6 and the oxidation state of cobalt is +3.

16. A coordination compound has the formula [Co(NH₃)₄Cl₂]Cl. Which of the following statements about this compound is INCORRECT?

A. The coordination number of cobalt in this compound is 6.
B. The primary valency of cobalt is satisfied by chloride ions outside the coordination sphere.
C. The compound exhibits ionization isomerism with [Co(NH₃)₄Cl(H₂O)]Cl₂.
D. The oxidation state of cobalt in this compound is +3.
Answer: C

The given compound [Co(NH₃)₄Cl₂]Cl has cobalt in the +3 oxidation state (option D is correct). The coordination number of cobalt is 6, as it is bonded to 4 NH₃ ligands and 2 Cl⁻ ligands (option A is correct). The primary valency (oxidation state) is satisfied by the chloride ion outside the coordination sphere (option B is correct). However, the compound does NOT exhibit ionization isomerism with [Co(NH₃)₄Cl(H₂O)]Cl₂ because the latter has a different coordination sphere (one Cl⁻ inside and one H₂O inside), making it a different compound, not an isomer. Ionization isomers must have the same coordination sphere but different counter ions. Thus, option C is incorrect.
