NEET Chemistry Practice: Redox Reactions (Part 1 of 2)

NEETEnglishPractice

This practice set contains 12 exam-focused multiple-choice questions on Redox Reactions, aligned with the NEET Chemistry syllabus. Each question carries +4 marks for a correct answer and -1 mark for an incorrect answer. Select the most appropriate option for each question.

1. In the reaction: 2FeCl₃ + SnCl₂ → 2FeCl₂ + SnCl₄, which species acts as the oxidizing agent?

A. FeCl₃
B. SnCl₂
C. FeCl₂
D. SnCl₄
Answer: A

An oxidizing agent gains electrons and gets reduced. In this reaction, Fe³⁺ in FeCl₃ is reduced to Fe²⁺ in FeCl₂, gaining one electron per iron atom. Thus, FeCl₃ acts as the oxidizing agent.

2. Which of the following statements is correct regarding the redox reaction: Zn + Cu²⁺ → Zn²⁺ + Cu?

A. Zinc is reduced and copper is oxidized.
B. Zinc is oxidized and copper is reduced.
C. Both zinc and copper are oxidized.
D. Both zinc and copper are reduced.
Answer: B

In this reaction, zinc (Zn) loses electrons to form Zn²⁺, so it is oxidized. Copper (Cu²⁺) gains electrons to form Cu, so it is reduced.

3. The equivalent weight of KMnO₄ in acidic medium is (M = molar mass of KMnO₄):

A. M/5
B. M/3
C. M/2
D. M
Answer: A

In acidic medium, KMnO₄ acts as a strong oxidizing agent and gains 5 electrons per molecule: MnO₄⁻ + 8H⁺ + 5e⁻ → Mn²⁺ + 4H₂O. Thus, the equivalent weight is M/5.

4. In the reaction: Cl₂ + 2NaOH → NaCl + NaClO + H₂O, which element undergoes disproportionation?

A. Chlorine
B. Sodium
C. Oxygen
D. Hydrogen
Answer: A

Disproportionation is a reaction where the same element is both oxidized and reduced. Here, chlorine is oxidized to NaClO (oxidation state +1) and reduced to NaCl (oxidation state -1).

5. What is the oxidation number of sulfur in H₂SO₅ (peroxymonosulfuric acid)?

A. +4
B. +6
C. +8
D. +2
Answer: B

In H₂SO₅, the structure includes a peroxide bond (O-O). The oxidation number of sulfur is calculated as: 2(+1) + x + 3(-2) + 2(-1) = 0 → 2 + x - 6 - 2 = 0 → x = +6.

6. The oxidation number of nitrogen in N₃H (hydrazoic acid) is:

A. -1/3
B. -1
C. +1/3
D. -1/2
Answer: A

Let the oxidation number of nitrogen be x. For N₃H: 3x + 1 = 0 → 3x = -1 → x = -1/3.

7. In the reaction: 3ClO⁻ → ClO₃⁻ + 2Cl⁻, the equivalent weight of ClO⁻ is (M = molar mass of ClO⁻):

A. M/2
B. M/3
C. M
D. M/4
Answer: A

In this disproportionation reaction, one ClO⁻ is oxidized to ClO₃⁻ (gains 4 electrons) and two ClO⁻ are reduced to Cl⁻ (lose 2 electrons each). The net change per ClO⁻ is 2 electrons, so the equivalent weight is M/2.

8. Which of the following reactions involves the oxidation of iron from +2 to +3 state?

A. Fe₂O₃ + 3CO → 2Fe + 3CO₂
B. 2FeCl₂ + Cl₂ → 2FeCl₃
C. Fe + 2HCl → FeCl₂ + H₂
D. Fe₃O₄ + 4H₂ → 3Fe + 4H₂O
Answer: B

In option B, Fe²⁺ in FeCl₂ is oxidized to Fe³⁺ in FeCl₃ by chlorine (Cl₂), which acts as the oxidizing agent.

9. The oxidation number of carbon in CH₂Cl₂ is:

A. 0
B. +2
C. -2
D. +4
Answer: A

Let the oxidation number of carbon be x. For CH₂Cl₂: x + 2(+1) + 2(-1) = 0 → x + 2 - 2 = 0 → x = 0.

10. In the redox titration of KMnO₄ against oxalic acid in acidic medium, which of the following is true?

A. KMnO₄ acts as a reducing agent.
B. Oxalic acid is oxidized to CO₂.
C. The reaction does not require an indicator.
D. The equivalent weight of oxalic acid is its molar mass.
Answer: B

In this titration, oxalic acid (C₂H₂O₄) is oxidized to CO₂, and KMnO₄ acts as the oxidizing agent. The reaction is self-indicating due to the color change of KMnO₄, but the primary fact is the oxidation of oxalic acid.

11. In the reaction: MnO₂ + 4HCl → MnCl₂ + Cl₂ + 2H₂O, how many electrons are transferred per mole of MnO₂?

A. 1
B. 2
C. 3
D. 4
Answer: B

Mn in MnO₂ has +4 oxidation state and is reduced to +2 in MnCl₂, gaining 2 electrons. Chlorine is oxidized from -1 in HCl to 0 in Cl₂, losing 1 electron per Cl atom. Two Cl atoms are oxidized, so 2 electrons are transferred per MnO₂.

12. Which statement about redox titrations is correct?

A. Potassium permanganate is used as a reducing agent in acidic medium.
B. In acidic medium, KMnO₄ oxidizes Fe²⁺ to Fe³⁺.
C. Sodium thiosulphate is commonly used to titrate iodine in basic medium.
D. The endpoint of a redox titration is always colorless.
Answer: B

In acidic medium, KMnO₄ acts as a strong oxidizing agent and oxidizes Fe²⁺ to Fe³⁺. Potassium permanganate is not a reducing agent (A is false). Sodium thiosulphate is used in neutral/acidic medium (C is false). The endpoint may be colored (D is false).

13. What is the equivalent weight of K₂Cr₂O₇ in acidic medium when it is reduced to Cr³⁺?

A. M/3
B. M/6
C. M/2
D. M
Answer: B

In acidic medium, K₂Cr₂O₇ is reduced to Cr³⁺. Each Cr atom gains 3 electrons (from +6 to +3). There are 2 Cr atoms, so total electrons gained = 6. Equivalent weight = Molar mass / 6 = M/6.

14. In the reaction: 3ClO⁻ → ClO₃⁻ + 2Cl⁻, which species is oxidized and which is reduced?

A. ClO⁻ is oxidized, ClO₃⁻ is reduced
B. ClO⁻ is both oxidized and reduced
C. ClO⁻ is reduced, Cl⁻ is oxidized
D. ClO₃⁻ is oxidized, Cl⁻ is reduced
Answer: B

This is a disproportionation reaction. Cl in ClO⁻ has +1 oxidation state. It is oxidized to ClO₃⁻ (+5) and reduced to Cl⁻ (-1). Thus, ClO⁻ is both oxidized and reduced.

15. What is the oxidation state of nitrogen in NH₄NO₃?

A. -3 in NH₄⁺ and +5 in NO₃⁻
B. -3 in both ions
C. +1 in NH₄⁺ and +5 in NO₃⁻
D. -3 in NH₄⁺ and -1 in NO₃⁻
Answer: A

In NH₄⁺, N has -3 oxidation state (x + 4(+1) = +1 → x = -3). In NO₃⁻, N has +5 oxidation state (x + 3(-2) = -1 → x = +5).

16. In the titration of oxalic acid with KMnO₄ in acidic medium, which indicator is typically used?

A. Phenolphthalein
B. Methyl orange
C. No external indicator
D. Starch
Answer: C

KMnO₄ acts as its own indicator. The endpoint is marked by the appearance of a permanent pink color due to excess KMnO₄.

17. Which of the following is the correct half-reaction for the reduction of H₂O₂ to H₂O in acidic medium?

A. H₂O₂ + 2H⁺ + 2e⁻ → 2H₂O
B. H₂O₂ → O₂ + 2H⁺ + 2e⁻
C. H₂O₂ + 2e⁻ → 2OH⁻
D. H₂O₂ + H⁺ + e⁻ → H₂O + OH⁻
Answer: A

In acidic medium, H₂O₂ is reduced to H₂O: H₂O₂ + 2H⁺ + 2e⁻ → 2H₂O. Option B is oxidation, C is in basic medium, and D is unbalanced.

18. What is the change in oxidation number of manganese when KMnO₄ is reduced to MnO₂ in neutral or faintly alkaline medium?

A. +7 to +4
B. +7 to +2
C. +6 to +4
D. +4 to +2
Answer: A

In KMnO₄, Mn has +7 oxidation state. In MnO₂, Mn has +4 oxidation state. The change is from +7 to +4.

19. In the redox reaction: MnO₄⁻ + I⁻ + H⁺ → Mn²⁺ + I₂ + H₂O, the equivalent weight of KMnO₄ (molar mass = 158 g/mol) when it acts as an oxidizing agent in acidic medium is:

A. 31.6 g/equiv
B. 52.7 g/equiv
C. 79.0 g/equiv
D. 158.0 g/equiv
Answer: A

In acidic medium, the permanganate ion (MnO₄⁻) is reduced to Mn²⁺, gaining 5 electrons per Mn atom: Mn⁺⁷ + 5e⁻ → Mn⁺². The equivalent weight of an oxidizing agent is given by its molar mass divided by the number of electrons gained per mole. Thus, equivalent weight of KMnO₄ = 158 g/mol ÷ 5 = 31.6 g/equiv. This calculation is critical for balancing redox reactions and determining stoichiometry in titrations.
