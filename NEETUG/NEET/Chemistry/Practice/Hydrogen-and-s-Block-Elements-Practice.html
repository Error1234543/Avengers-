This practice set contains 12 multiple-choice questions (MCQs) focused on Hydrogen and s-Block Elements, aligned with the NEET exam pattern. Each question carries +4 marks for a correct answer and -1 mark for an incorrect answer. Select the most appropriate option for each question.

1. Which of the following properties is NOT characteristic of alkali metals?

A. They have low ionization enthalpies.
B. They exhibit a +1 oxidation state in their compounds.
C. They form peroxides and superoxides when reacting with excess oxygen.
D. They are stored under water to prevent oxidation.
Answer: D

Alkali metals are highly reactive and react vigorously with water, producing hydrogen gas and hydroxides. They are stored under kerosene or mineral oil, not water, to prevent oxidation. The other options are correct: alkali metals have low ionization enthalpies, exhibit a +1 oxidation state, and form peroxides (e.g., Na2O2) and superoxides (e.g., KO2) with excess oxygen.

2. The correct statement about the solubility of alkaline earth metal hydroxides is:

A. Solubility increases from Be(OH)2 to Ba(OH)2.
B. Solubility decreases from Be(OH)2 to Ba(OH)2.
C. All alkaline earth metal hydroxides are equally soluble.
D. Be(OH)2 is the most soluble among the group.
Answer: A

The solubility of alkaline earth metal hydroxides increases down the group due to the decreasing lattice enthalpy and increasing hydration enthalpy. Thus, Be(OH)2 is the least soluble, and Ba(OH)2 is the most soluble.

3. Which of the following reactions is used for the industrial preparation of sodium hydroxide?

A. Electrolysis of molten sodium chloride.
B. Electrolysis of aqueous sodium chloride using a mercury cathode.
C. Reaction of sodium carbonate with calcium hydroxide.
D. Thermal decomposition of sodium bicarbonate.
Answer: B

Sodium hydroxide is industrially prepared by the electrolysis of aqueous sodium chloride (brine) using a mercury cathode in the Castner-Kellner cell. This process produces sodium hydroxide, chlorine gas, and hydrogen gas. Option A describes the preparation of sodium metal, not sodium hydroxide. Option C is the Leblanc process, which is outdated, and Option D produces sodium carbonate, not sodium hydroxide.

4. The correct statement about the reaction of alkaline earth metals with water is:

A. All alkaline earth metals react vigorously with water at room temperature.
B. Beryllium does not react with water, even at high temperatures.
C. Magnesium reacts with cold water to form magnesium hydroxide and hydrogen gas.
D. Calcium reacts with water more vigorously than magnesium.
Answer: D

Calcium reacts more vigorously with water than magnesium, producing calcium hydroxide and hydrogen gas. Beryllium does not react with water, even at high temperatures, due to the formation of a protective oxide layer. Magnesium reacts very slowly with cold water but more readily with steam. Thus, the correct statement is that calcium reacts more vigorously than magnesium.

5. Which of the following is NOT a use of sodium bicarbonate?

A. As an antacid to neutralize stomach acid.
B. As a fire extinguisher for electrical fires.
C. As a leavening agent in baking.
D. As a mild abrasive in toothpaste.
Answer: B

Sodium bicarbonate (NaHCO3) is used as an antacid, a leavening agent in baking, and a mild abrasive in toothpaste. However, it is not used in fire extinguishers for electrical fires; sodium carbonate or specialized extinguishing agents are used instead to avoid conductivity issues.

6. Which of the following statements about the solubility of sulfates of alkaline earth metals is correct?

A. Solubility increases from BeSO4 to BaSO4.
B. Solubility decreases from BeSO4 to BaSO4.
C. All sulfates of alkaline earth metals are equally soluble.
D. BeSO4 is insoluble in water.
Answer: B

The solubility of sulfates of alkaline earth metals decreases down the group. BeSO4 is highly soluble, while BaSO4 is nearly insoluble. Thus, the correct statement is that solubility decreases from BeSO4 to BaSO4.

7. Which of the following statements about the position of hydrogen in the periodic table is INCORRECT?

A. Hydrogen resembles alkali metals in its ability to lose one electron to form H⁺.
B. Hydrogen resembles halogens in its ability to gain one electron to form H⁻.
C. Hydrogen is placed in Group 1 due to its electronic configuration 1s¹, similar to alkali metals.
D. Hydrogen is a non-metal, so it is placed in Group 17 along with halogens.
Answer: D

Hydrogen is unique and does not fit perfectly into any group. While it resembles alkali metals (Group 1) due to its 1s¹ configuration and ability to form H⁺, and halogens (Group 17) due to its ability to form H⁻, it is not placed in Group 17 because it is not a halogen. Its placement in Group 1 is based on its electronic configuration, not its non-metallic nature.

8. Which of the following properties of water is NOT due to hydrogen bonding?

A. High boiling point compared to H₂S
B. High specific heat capacity
C. Low density of ice compared to liquid water
D. High electrical conductivity
Answer: D

Water's high boiling point, high specific heat capacity, and the low density of ice are all due to hydrogen bonding. However, pure water has very low electrical conductivity because it is a poor conductor of electricity. Electrical conductivity in water is due to dissolved ions, not hydrogen bonding.

9. In the preparation of sodium hydroxide by the Castner-Kellner cell, which of the following is used as the cathode?

A. Graphite
B. Mercury
C. Iron
D. Platinum
Answer: B

In the Castner-Kellner cell, mercury is used as the cathode. Sodium ions are discharged at the mercury cathode to form sodium amalgam, which then reacts with water to produce sodium hydroxide.

10. Which of the following statements about the solubility of Group 2 hydroxides is correct?

A. Solubility increases down the group from Be(OH)₂ to Ba(OH)₂.
B. Solubility decreases down the group from Be(OH)₂ to Ba(OH)₂.
C. All Group 2 hydroxides are highly soluble in water.
D. Solubility is independent of the cation size in Group 2 hydroxides.
Answer: A

The solubility of Group 2 hydroxides increases down the group due to the decreasing lattice enthalpy and increasing hydration enthalpy of the larger cations. Thus, Be(OH)₂ is the least soluble, and Ba(OH)₂ is the most soluble.

11. The reaction of lithium with nitrogen forms:

A. Li₃N
B. LiN₃
C. LiNO₃
D. Li₂N
Answer: A

Lithium is the only alkali metal that reacts directly with nitrogen to form lithium nitride (Li₃N). This is due to the small size of lithium, which allows it to form a stable nitride.

12. Which of the following is the correct order of basic strength of the oxides of Group 2 elements?

A. BeO < MgO < CaO < SrO < BaO
B. BaO < SrO < CaO < MgO < BeO
C. BeO < CaO < MgO < SrO < BaO
D. MgO < BeO < CaO < SrO < BaO
Answer: A

The basic strength of the oxides of Group 2 elements increases down the group. BeO is amphoteric, while MgO, CaO, SrO, and BaO are basic, with basicity increasing as the size of the cation increases. Thus, the order is BeO < MgO < CaO < SrO < BaO.

13. In the Solvay process, which of the following is NOT a by-product?

A. Calcium chloride
B. Ammonium chloride
C. Sodium carbonate
D. Carbon dioxide
Answer: C

In the Solvay process, sodium carbonate (Na₂CO₃) is the main product, not a by-product. The by-products include calcium chloride (CaCl₂), ammonium chloride (NH₄Cl), and carbon dioxide (CO₂) is recycled in the process.

14. The correct order of increasing reducing power of the alkali metals is:

A. Li < Na < K < Rb < Cs
B. Cs < Rb < K < Na < Li
C. Na < Li < K < Rb < Cs
D. Li < K < Na < Rb < Cs
Answer: A

The reducing power of alkali metals increases down the group due to the decreasing ionization enthalpy, which makes it easier for the metal to lose an electron. However, lithium is an exception due to its high hydration enthalpy, making it the strongest reducing agent in aqueous solutions. In the gaseous state, the order is Li < Na < K < Rb < Cs.

15. Which of the following is used as a drying agent for neutral gases like nitrogen and oxygen?

A. Calcium chloride
B. Sodium hydroxide
C. Phosphorus pentoxide
D. Concentrated sulfuric acid
Answer: A

Calcium chloride (CaCl₂) is commonly used as a drying agent for neutral gases like nitrogen and oxygen because it is hygroscopic and does not react with these gases. Sodium hydroxide and concentrated sulfuric acid are corrosive and react with many gases, while phosphorus pentoxide is used for acidic gases.

16. The correct order of increasing thermal stability of the carbonates of Group 2 elements is:

A. BeCO₃ < MgCO₃ < CaCO₃ < SrCO₃ < BaCO₃
B. BaCO₃ < SrCO₃ < CaCO₃ < MgCO₃ < BeCO₃
C. MgCO₃ < BeCO₃ < CaCO₃ < SrCO₃ < BaCO₃
D. BeCO₃ < CaCO₃ < MgCO₃ < SrCO₃ < BaCO₃
Answer: A

The thermal stability of Group 2 carbonates increases down the group. This is due to the increasing size of the cation, which leads to a decrease in the polarizing power of the cation and thus greater stability of the carbonate. The order is BeCO₃ < MgCO₃ < CaCO₃ < SrCO₃ < BaCO₃.
