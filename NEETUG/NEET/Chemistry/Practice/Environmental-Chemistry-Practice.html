1. The Montreal Protocol is an international treaty designed to phase out the production of which substances?

A. Greenhouse gases like CO₂
B. Ozone-depleting substances like CFCs
C. Heavy metals like mercury
D. Plastic waste
Answer: B

The Montreal Protocol (1987) is an international agreement aimed at phasing out ozone-depleting substances (ODS) such as chlorofluorocarbons (CFCs), halons, and other related compounds. It has been highly successful in reducing the depletion of the ozone layer.

2. The Greenhouse Effect is essential for life on Earth because it:

A. Prevents all solar radiation from reaching the Earth's surface.
B. Traps heat in the atmosphere, maintaining Earth's average temperature.
C. Causes the depletion of the ozone layer.
D. Increases the concentration of oxygen in the atmosphere.
Answer: B

The Greenhouse Effect is a natural process where greenhouse gases (e.g., CO₂, CH₄, water vapor) trap heat in the Earth's atmosphere, maintaining the planet's average temperature at a level suitable for life. Without it, Earth would be too cold to support most forms of life.

3. The pH of normal rainwater is slightly acidic due to the dissolution of:

A. Sulfur dioxide (SO2) and nitrogen oxides (NOx)
B. Carbon dioxide (CO2) forming carbonic acid
C. Ozone (O3) from photochemical reactions
D. Ammonia (NH3) from agricultural activities
Answer: B

Normal rainwater has a pH of about 5.6 due to the dissolution of atmospheric CO2, which forms carbonic acid (H2CO3). Acid rain, however, has a pH below 5.6 and is caused by SO2 and NOx.

4. Biochemical Oxygen Demand (BOD) is a measure of:

A. The amount of oxygen required by aquatic organisms to survive
B. The amount of oxygen consumed by microorganisms to decompose organic matter in water
C. The concentration of dissolved oxygen in water
D. The rate of photosynthesis in aquatic plants
Answer: B

BOD measures the amount of dissolved oxygen required by aerobic microorganisms to decompose organic matter in water. High BOD indicates high organic pollution, which can deplete oxygen levels and harm aquatic life.

5. Which of the following is NOT a method for controlling particulate matter pollution?

A. Electrostatic precipitators
B. Catalytic converters
C. Scrubbers
D. Cyclone separators
Answer: B

Catalytic converters are used to reduce gaseous pollutants like CO, NOx, and hydrocarbons in vehicle emissions, not particulate matter. Electrostatic precipitators, scrubbers, and cyclone separators are specifically designed to control particulate pollution.

6. The 'Greenhouse Effect' is primarily caused by:

A. The reflection of solar radiation by clouds
B. The absorption and re-emission of infrared radiation by greenhouse gases
C. The depletion of the ozone layer
D. The increase in solar activity
Answer: B

The greenhouse effect occurs when greenhouse gases (e.g., CO2, CH4) absorb and re-emit infrared radiation, trapping heat in the Earth's atmosphere and leading to global warming.

7. Which of the following statements correctly explains the formation of photochemical smog?

A. It is formed by the reaction of nitrogen oxides and volatile organic compounds (VOCs) in the presence of sunlight, leading to the production of ozone and peroxyacetyl nitrate (PAN).
B. It is primarily caused by the combustion of sulfur-containing fuels, resulting in sulfur dioxide emissions that react with water vapor to form sulfuric acid.
C. It occurs due to the excessive release of carbon monoxide from incomplete combustion, which reacts with atmospheric oxygen to form carbon dioxide.
D. It is a result of the reaction between chlorofluorocarbons (CFCs) and ozone in the stratosphere, leading to ozone depletion.
Answer: A

Photochemical smog is formed when nitrogen oxides (NOx) and volatile organic compounds (VOCs) react in the presence of sunlight. This reaction produces secondary pollutants such as ozone (O3) and peroxyacetyl nitrate (PAN), which are harmful to human health and the environment. Option B describes acid rain formation, Option C refers to carbon monoxide pollution, and Option D pertains to ozone layer depletion, not photochemical smog.

8. Which of the following statements correctly describes the role of chlorofluorocarbons (CFCs) in the depletion of the ozone layer?

A. CFCs directly react with ozone molecules in the troposphere, breaking them down into oxygen.
B. CFCs release chlorine atoms upon photodissociation in the stratosphere, which catalytically destroy ozone molecules.
C. CFCs absorb ultraviolet radiation and convert it into heat, thereby reducing the formation of ozone.
D. CFCs react with nitrogen oxides in the stratosphere to form ozone-depleting compounds like chlorine nitrate.
Answer: B

Chlorofluorocarbons (CFCs) are stable in the troposphere but undergo photodissociation in the stratosphere due to ultraviolet radiation. This process releases chlorine atoms, which catalytically destroy ozone molecules by converting them into oxygen. Each chlorine atom can destroy thousands of ozone molecules, leading to ozone layer depletion. This mechanism is well-documented and is the primary reason for the formation of the ozone hole, particularly over Antarctica.
