NEET Chemistry Practice: Polymers (Part 1 of 2)

NEETEnglishPractice

This practice set contains 12 multiple-choice questions on Polymers, aligned with the NEET Chemistry syllabus and exam pattern. Each question has four options, only one of which is correct. Select the most appropriate answer. Negative marking applies as per NEET rules (+4/-1).

1. The polymer used in making non-stick cookware is:

A. Polyvinyl chloride (PVC)
B. Polytetrafluoroethylene (PTFE)
C. Polystyrene
D. Polyethylene terephthalate (PET)
Answer: B

Polytetrafluoroethylene (PTFE), commonly known as Teflon, is used in non-stick cookware due to its high thermal stability, chemical inertness, and low friction coefficient.

2. The repeating unit in natural rubber is:

A. Isoprene
B. Chloroprene
C. Neoprene
D. Styrene
Answer: A

Natural rubber is a polymer of isoprene (2-methyl-1,3-butadiene). Its repeating unit is derived from isoprene monomers linked in a cis-1,4 configuration.

3. The process of vulcanization involves the addition of which element to natural rubber?

A. Oxygen
B. Sulfur
C. Nitrogen
D. Carbon
Answer: B

Vulcanization is the process of heating natural rubber with sulfur to improve its elasticity, strength, and thermal stability by forming cross-links between polymer chains.

4. Which of the following is an example of a thermoplastic polymer?

A. Bakelite
B. Melamine-formaldehyde resin
C. Polyethylene
D. Urea-formaldehyde resin
Answer: C

Thermoplastic polymers, like polyethylene, soften on heating and harden on cooling, making them recyclable. Bakelite and urea-formaldehyde resins are thermosetting polymers, which do not soften upon reheating.

5. The polymer used in making disposable cups and packaging materials is:

A. Polystyrene
B. Polypropylene
C. Polyvinyl chloride (PVC)
D. Polyethylene terephthalate (PET)
Answer: A

Polystyrene is widely used in disposable cups, packaging materials, and insulation due to its lightweight, rigidity, and low cost.

6. Which of the following polymers is classified as an elastomer?

A. Bakelite
B. Neoprene
C. Polystyrene
D. Polyethylene
Answer: B

Elastomers are polymers with high elasticity and flexibility, such as neoprene (a synthetic rubber). Bakelite and polystyrene are rigid polymers, while polyethylene is a thermoplastic with limited elasticity.

7. The polymer used in making electrical switches and handles of utensils is:

A. Bakelite
B. Polyethylene
C. Polystyrene
D. Polyvinyl chloride (PVC)
Answer: A

Bakelite is a thermosetting phenol-formaldehyde resin known for its electrical insulating properties and heat resistance, making it suitable for electrical switches and utensil handles.

8. Which of the following polymers is thermosetting?

A. Polyethylene
B. Bakelite
C. Polyvinyl chloride (PVC)
D. Polystyrene
Answer: B

Bakelite is a thermosetting polymer that, once molded, cannot be remelted or reshaped. Polyethylene, PVC, and polystyrene are thermoplastic polymers, which can be remelted and reshaped.

9. The polymer used in the manufacture of disposable plastic cups is:

A. Polyethylene terephthalate (PET)
B. High-density polyethylene (HDPE)
C. Polystyrene
D. Polypropylene
Answer: C

Polystyrene is commonly used in the manufacture of disposable plastic cups due to its lightweight, insulating properties, and low cost. PET is used in bottles, HDPE in containers, and polypropylene in reusable containers.

10. The polymer formed by the polymerization of methyl methacrylate is:

A. Plexiglas
B. Bakelite
C. Melamine
D. Teflon
Answer: A

Plexiglas, also known as polymethyl methacrylate (PMMA), is formed by the polymerization of methyl methacrylate. It is a transparent, lightweight, and shatter-resistant polymer used in place of glass.

11. The polymer used in the manufacture of electrical switches is:

A. Bakelite
B. Polyethylene
C. Polystyrene
D. Polyvinyl chloride (PVC)
Answer: A

Bakelite is a thermosetting polymer used in electrical switches and insulators due to its excellent electrical insulating properties and heat resistance. Polyethylene, polystyrene, and PVC are not typically used for this purpose.

12. A polymer sample exhibits the following properties: high tensile strength, resistance to abrasion, and elasticity. It is commonly used in the manufacture of tyres and conveyor belts. Identify the polymer from the options below.

A. Polyvinyl chloride (PVC)
B. Styrene-butadiene rubber (SBR)
C. Polymethyl methacrylate (PMMA)
D. Urea-formaldehyde resin
Answer: B

Styrene-butadiene rubber (SBR) is a synthetic rubber known for its high tensile strength, resistance to abrasion, and elasticity. It is widely used in the manufacture of tyres, conveyor belts, and other industrial applications. PVC is rigid and used in pipes, PMMA is a transparent plastic used in sheets, and urea-formaldehyde resin is a thermosetting polymer used in adhesives.

13. The process of vulcanisation involves the addition of which element to natural rubber to improve its properties such as elasticity and resistance to heat?

A. Oxygen
B. Nitrogen
C. Sulphur
D. Chlorine
Answer: C

Vulcanisation is the process of adding sulphur to natural rubber to form cross-links between the polymer chains. This improves the elasticity, tensile strength, and resistance to heat and abrasion of the rubber. Oxygen, nitrogen, and chlorine are not used in the vulcanisation process.
