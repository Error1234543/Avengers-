This practice set contains 12 exam-focused multiple-choice questions on Chemical Bonding and Molecular Structure, aligned with the NEET syllabus and difficulty level. 1. The correct order of bond lengths for the following bonds is:

A. C≡C < C=C < C-C
B. C-C < C=C < C≡C
C. C=C < C≡C < C-C
D. C≡C < C-C < C=C
Answer: A

Bond length decreases with increasing bond order. A triple bond (C≡C) is shorter than a double bond (C=C), which is shorter than a single bond (C-C). Thus, the order is C≡C < C=C < C-C.

2. The correct statement regarding the polarity of molecules is:

A. All diatomic molecules are polar
B. A molecule with polar bonds is always polar
C. A symmetrical molecule with polar bonds can be non-polar
D. Non-polar molecules cannot have polar bonds
Answer: C

A symmetrical molecule with polar bonds (e.g., CO₂) can be non-polar if the bond dipoles cancel each other out. Not all diatomic molecules are polar (e.g., H₂, Cl₂). A molecule with polar bonds is not always polar (e.g., CCl₄). Non-polar molecules can have polar bonds if the geometry cancels the dipole moment.

3. The correct order of increasing bond length among C-C, C=C, and C≡C bonds is:

A. C≡C < C=C < C-C
B. C-C < C=C < C≡C
C. C=C < C≡C < C-C
D. C-C < C≡C < C=C
Answer: A

Bond length decreases with increasing bond order. A triple bond (C≡C) is shorter than a double bond (C=C), which is shorter than a single bond (C-C).

4. Which of the following statements about hydrogen bonding is incorrect?

A. It is stronger than van der Waals forces but weaker than covalent bonds.
B. It occurs only in molecules containing hydrogen bonded to highly electronegative atoms like N, O, or F.
C. It is responsible for the high boiling point of water.
D. It can occur between molecules of the same type or different types.
Answer: B

While hydrogen bonding typically involves H bonded to N, O, or F, it can also occur with other electronegative atoms like Cl in certain cases (e.g., chloroform). The other statements are correct.

5. The shape of the XeF₄ molecule is:

A. Tetrahedral
B. Square planar
C. See-saw
D. Octahedral
Answer: B

XeF₄ has 4 bonding pairs and 2 lone pairs of electrons, resulting in a square planar geometry due to sp³d² hybridization.

6. Which of the following is a polar molecule?

A. BCl₃
B. CCl₄
C. NH₃
D. CO₂
Answer: C

NH₃ has a trigonal pyramidal shape with a net dipole moment due to the lone pair on nitrogen. The other molecules are symmetric and non-polar.

7. Which of the following molecules does NOT obey the octet rule?

A. CO₂
B. BF₃
C. NH₃
D. H₂O
Answer: B

BF₃ has only 6 electrons around boron, violating the octet rule. The other molecules satisfy the octet rule.

8. The molecule with the strongest van der Waals forces among the following is:

A. CH₄
B. C₂H₆
C. C₃H₈
D. C₄H₁₀
Answer: D

Van der Waals forces increase with molecular size and surface area. C₄H₁₀ (butane) has the largest size among the given options, resulting in the strongest van der Waals forces.

9. The bond angle in NH₃ is greater than that in H₂O, but less than the tetrahedral angle (109°28'). Which of the following statements correctly explains this observation?

A. The lone pair-lone pair repulsion in H₂O is stronger than the lone pair-bond pair repulsion in NH₃, reducing the bond angle in H₂O.
B. The bond pair-bond pair repulsion in NH₃ is stronger than in H₂O, increasing the bond angle in NH₃.
C. The electronegativity of oxygen is higher than nitrogen, which pulls the bonding electrons closer and reduces the bond angle in H₂O.
D. NH₃ has a trigonal planar geometry, while H₂O has a bent geometry, leading to a larger bond angle in NH₃.
Answer: A

The bond angle in NH₃ (107°) is greater than in H₂O (104.5°) but less than the ideal tetrahedral angle due to the presence of lone pairs. In NH₃, there is one lone pair, which exerts lone pair-bond pair repulsion, compressing the bond angle slightly below 109°28'. In H₂O, there are two lone pairs, leading to stronger lone pair-lone pair repulsion, which further compresses the bond angle. While electronegativity (Option C) does play a role in bond polarity, the primary factor here is the repulsion between lone pairs and bonding pairs. Option D is incorrect because neither NH₃ nor H₂O has a trigonal planar geometry; both are based on a tetrahedral arrangement with lone pairs.

10. Which of the following molecules exhibits sp³d hybridization and has a trigonal bipyramidal geometry?

A. PCl₅
B. SF₆
C. IF₇
D. XeF₄
Answer: A

PCl₅ exhibits sp³d hybridization due to the presence of five bond pairs around the central phosphorus atom. This results in a trigonal bipyramidal geometry. SF₆ has sp³d² hybridization and octahedral geometry, IF₇ has sp³d³ hybridization and pentagonal bipyramidal geometry, while XeF₄ has sp³d² hybridization and square planar geometry.

11. The bond angle in H₂O is less than that in NH₃ because:

A. Oxygen has a higher electronegativity than nitrogen
B. The lone pair-lone pair repulsion in H₂O is greater than the lone pair-bond pair repulsion in NH₃
C. NH₃ has a pyramidal shape while H₂O is linear
D. The bond length in H₂O is shorter than in NH₃
Answer: B

In H₂O, there are two lone pairs on the oxygen atom, leading to greater lone pair-lone pair repulsion compared to the lone pair-bond pair repulsion in NH₃ (which has only one lone pair). This increased repulsion compresses the bond angle in H₂O (104.5°) compared to NH₃ (107°). The other options are incorrect: NH₃ is pyramidal, not linear, and bond length does not directly determine bond angle.

12. A molecule with the formula AB₄ has a see-saw shape. Which of the following is true regarding its hybridization and lone pairs?

A. sp³d hybridization with one lone pair
B. sp³ hybridization with no lone pairs
C. sp³d² hybridization with two lone pairs
D. sp² hybridization with one lone pair
Answer: A

A see-saw shape is characteristic of sp³d hybridization with one lone pair on the central atom (e.g., SF₄). The lone pair occupies an equatorial position to minimize repulsion, resulting in the see-saw geometry. sp³ hybridization would yield tetrahedral geometry, sp³d² would yield octahedral or square planar, and sp² would yield trigonal planar.
