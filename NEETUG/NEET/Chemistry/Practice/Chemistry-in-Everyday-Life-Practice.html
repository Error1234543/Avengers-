NEET Chemistry Practice: Chemistry in Everyday Life (Part 1 of 2)

NEETEnglishPractice

This practice set contains 12 multiple-choice questions (MCQs) focused on Chemistry in Everyday Life, aligned with the NEET exam pattern. Each question carries +4 marks for a correct answer and -1 mark for an incorrect answer. Select the most appropriate answer for each question.

1. Which of the following drugs is classified as a tranquilizer?

A. Equanil
B. Seconal
C. Luminal
D. Morphine
Answer: A

Equanil (meprobamate) is a well-known tranquilizer used to reduce anxiety and tension. Seconal and Luminal are barbiturates (hypnotics), while morphine is a narcotic analgesic.

2. Which class of drugs is used to treat peptic ulcers by reducing gastric acid secretion?

A. Antihistamines
B. Antacids
C. Proton pump inhibitors
D. Antimicrobials
Answer: C

Proton pump inhibitors (e.g., omeprazole) block the enzyme responsible for acid secretion in the stomach, making them highly effective for treating peptic ulcers. Antacids neutralize acid but do not reduce secretion.

3. The structure of chloramphenicol contains which of the following functional groups?

A. Nitro group
B. Sulfonamide group
C. Carboxyl group
D. Amino group only
Answer: A

Chloramphenicol is a broad-spectrum antibiotic containing a nitrobenzene ring, which includes a nitro (-NO₂) functional group. It does not contain a sulfonamide or carboxyl group.

4. Soaps are sodium or potassium salts of long-chain fatty acids. Which of the following statements about soaps is INCORRECT?

A. Soaps are biodegradable.
B. Soaps form scum in hard water.
C. Soaps are effective in acidic solutions.
D. Soaps are prepared by saponification of fats.
Answer: C

Soaps are ineffective in acidic solutions because they form insoluble fatty acids. They are biodegradable, form scum in hard water due to calcium/magnesium ions, and are prepared via saponification.

5. Which of the following is a cationic detergent commonly used in hair conditioners?

A. Sodium lauryl sulfate
B. Cetyltrimethylammonium bromide (CTAB)
C. Sodium stearate
D. Sodium dodecylbenzenesulfonate
Answer: B

Cetyltrimethylammonium bromide (CTAB) is a cationic detergent used in hair conditioners due to its positive charge, which helps reduce static and improve manageability. The others are anionic detergents.

6. The drug paracetamol is primarily used for which of the following purposes?

A. Antibiotic
B. Analgesic and antipyretic
C. Antihistamine
D. Antidepressant
Answer: B

Paracetamol (acetaminophen) is widely used as an analgesic (pain reliever) and antipyretic (fever reducer). It is not an antibiotic, antihistamine, or antidepressant.

7. Which of the following statements about antibiotics is TRUE?

A. Antibiotics are effective against viral infections.
B. Penicillin acts by inhibiting bacterial cell wall synthesis.
C. Broad-spectrum antibiotics target only Gram-positive bacteria.
D. Antibiotics should be used to treat the common cold.
Answer: B

Penicillin inhibits bacterial cell wall synthesis, making it effective against many bacterial infections. Antibiotics are ineffective against viruses, broad-spectrum antibiotics target multiple bacteria types, and they should not be used for viral illnesses like the common cold.

8. Which of the following is a synthetic detergent that does NOT form scum in hard water?

A. Sodium palmitate
B. Sodium lauryl sulfate
C. Sodium stearate
D. Sodium oleate
Answer: B

Sodium lauryl sulfate is a synthetic detergent that does not form scum in hard water because it does not precipitate with calcium or magnesium ions. The others are soaps and form scum.

9. The drug ranitidine is classified as which of the following?

A. Antacid
B. H₂-receptor antagonist
C. Proton pump inhibitor
D. Antibiotic
Answer: B

Ranitidine is an H₂-receptor antagonist that reduces gastric acid secretion by blocking histamine H₂ receptors. It is not an antacid, proton pump inhibitor, or antibiotic.

10. Which of the following is a common preservative used in food and pharmaceuticals?

A. Sodium benzoate
B. Aspartame
C. Saccharin
D. Alitame
Answer: A

Sodium benzoate is a widely used preservative in foods and pharmaceuticals to inhibit microbial growth. Aspartame, saccharin, and alitame are artificial sweeteners, not preservatives.

11. Which of the following is a non-narcotic analgesic commonly used to relieve pain and reduce fever?

A. Morphine
B. Aspirin
C. Penicillin
D. Chloramphenicol
Answer: B

Aspirin (acetylsalicylic acid) is a non-narcotic analgesic and antipyretic drug used to relieve pain and reduce fever. Morphine is a narcotic analgesic, while penicillin and chloramphenicol are antibiotics.

12. The class of drugs used to treat stress and mental diseases by correcting the imbalance of neurotransmitters in the brain is called:

A. Antacids
B. Antihistamines
C. Tranquilizers
D. Antibiotics
Answer: C

Tranquilizers are drugs that treat stress, anxiety, and mental disorders by balancing neurotransmitters like serotonin and dopamine in the brain. Antacids neutralize stomach acid, antihistamines treat allergies, and antibiotics kill bacteria.

13. Which of the following is NOT a function of antiseptics?

A. Kill or inhibit the growth of microorganisms on living tissues
B. Prevent infection in cuts and wounds
C. Treat systemic bacterial infections
D. Used in mouthwashes and hand sanitizers
Answer: C

Antiseptics are applied to living tissues to kill or inhibit microorganisms and prevent infections. They are not used to treat systemic (internal) bacterial infections, which require antibiotics.

14. Sulpha drugs, such as sulphanilamide, are structural analogs of:

A. Para-aminobenzoic acid (PABA)
B. Folic acid
C. Penicillin
D. Tetracycline
Answer: A

Sulpha drugs are structural analogs of para-aminobenzoic acid (PABA), a compound essential for bacterial synthesis of folic acid. By mimicking PABA, sulpha drugs inhibit bacterial growth.

15. The artificial sweetener that is unstable at cooking temperatures and is often used in cold foods and soft drinks is:

A. Saccharin
B. Aspartame
C. Sucralose
D. Alitame
Answer: B

Aspartame is an artificial sweetener that is unstable at high temperatures and is commonly used in cold foods, beverages, and soft drinks. Saccharin, sucralose, and alitame are more heat-stable.

16. Which of the following detergents is biodegradable and does not cause water pollution?

A. Sodium lauryl sulphate
B. Sodium dodecylbenzenesulphonate (branched chain)
C. Linear alkylbenzenesulphonates (LAS)
D. Cetyltrimethylammonium bromide
Answer: C

Linear alkylbenzenesulphonates (LAS) are biodegradable detergents that break down naturally and do not cause significant water pollution. Branched-chain detergents like sodium dodecylbenzenesulphonate are non-biodegradable and cause pollution.

17. The drug cimetidine is classified as a/an:

A. Antibiotic
B. Antacid
C. Antihistamine (H2-receptor antagonist)
D. Analgesic
Answer: C

Cimetidine is an antihistamine that acts as an H2-receptor antagonist, reducing stomach acid production to treat ulcers and acid reflux. It is not an antibiotic, antacid, or analgesic.

18. The class of drugs that mimic the natural chemical messengers of the body and bind to specific receptors to produce a biological response are called:

A. Antagonists
B. Agonists
C. Enzyme inhibitors
D. Antimetabolites
Answer: B

Agonists are drugs that mimic natural chemical messengers (e.g., hormones or neurotransmitters) and bind to receptors to produce a biological response. Antagonists block receptors, while enzyme inhibitors and antimetabolites interfere with metabolic processes.

19. Which of the following is a preservative commonly used in food and pharmaceuticals to prevent microbial growth?

A. Sodium benzoate
B. Aspartame
C. Saccharin
D. BHT (Butylated hydroxytoluene)
Answer: A

Sodium benzoate is a preservative used in foods, beverages, and pharmaceuticals to inhibit microbial growth. Aspartame and saccharin are sweeteners, while BHT is an antioxidant.

20. The drug ranitidine is used to:

A. Relieve pain and inflammation
B. Treat bacterial infections
C. Reduce stomach acid production
D. Lower blood pressure
Answer: C

Ranitidine is an H2-receptor antagonist that reduces stomach acid production, used to treat ulcers, acid reflux, and heartburn. It does not relieve pain, treat infections, or lower blood pressure.
