PYQ-STYLE PRACTICE — NEET PYQ-STYLE PRACTICE: d and f Block Elements (Part 1 of 2)

NEETEnglishPYQ-Style Practice

This section contains 12 multiple-choice questions (MCQs) based on the NEET exam pattern for the topic 'd and f Block Elements'. Each question carries 4 marks, with a negative marking of -1 for incorrect answers. Select the correct option for each question.

1. Which of the following transition metals exhibits the highest oxidation state?

A. Scandium (Sc)
B. Manganese (Mn)
C. Iron (Fe)
D. Copper (Cu)
Answer: B

Manganese (Mn) exhibits the highest oxidation state of +7 in its compounds like KMnO₄, which is the maximum among the given options. Scandium typically shows +3, Iron +6 (rarely), and Copper +2 or +1.

2. The color of transition metal complexes is primarily due to:

A. d-d transitions
B. s-p transitions
C. n-π* transitions
D. π-π* transitions
Answer: A

The color of transition metal complexes arises from d-d electronic transitions, where electrons in the d-orbitals absorb specific wavelengths of light and reflect the complementary color.

3. Which of the following lanthanoid ions is diamagnetic?

A. Ce³⁺
B. Gd³⁺
C. Lu³⁺
D. Nd³⁺
Answer: C

Lu³⁺ has a fully filled 4f¹⁴ configuration, making it diamagnetic. Ce³⁺ (4f¹), Gd³⁺ (4f⁷), and Nd³⁺ (4f³) have unpaired electrons and are paramagnetic.

4. The correct order of stability for the +2 oxidation state among the following first-row transition metals is:

A. Mn²⁺ > Fe²⁺ > Co²⁺ > Ni²⁺
B. Fe²⁺ > Co²⁺ > Ni²⁺ > Mn²⁺
C. Ni²⁺ > Co²⁺ > Fe²⁺ > Mn²⁺
D. Co²⁺ > Ni²⁺ > Fe²⁺ > Mn²⁺
Answer: C

The stability of the +2 oxidation state increases across the first-row transition metals due to the increasing nuclear charge and decreasing ionic radius. Ni²⁺ is the most stable, followed by Co²⁺, Fe²⁺, and Mn²⁺.

5. Which of the following statements about lanthanoid contraction is INCORRECT?

A. It causes the atomic radii of Zr and Hf to be nearly identical.
B. It results from the poor shielding effect of 4f electrons.
C. It leads to a decrease in the basicity of lanthanoid hydroxides with increasing atomic number.
D. It is responsible for the larger size of lanthanoids compared to actinoids.
Answer: D

Lanthanoid contraction refers to the gradual decrease in atomic and ionic radii across the lanthanoid series due to poor shielding of 4f electrons. Actinoids are generally larger than lanthanoids, so statement D is incorrect.

6. The magnetic moment of a transition metal ion with 3 unpaired electrons is approximately:

A. 1.73 BM
B. 3.87 BM
C. 4.90 BM
D. 5.92 BM
Answer: B

The magnetic moment (μ) is calculated using the formula μ = √[n(n+2)] BM, where n is the number of unpaired electrons. For n = 3, μ = √[3(5)] = √15 ≈ 3.87 BM.

7. Which of the following actinoids is NOT found naturally and is entirely synthetic?

A. Uranium (U)
B. Plutonium (Pu)
C. Americium (Am)
D. Thorium (Th)
Answer: C

Americium (Am) is a synthetic actinoid and does not occur naturally. Uranium, Plutonium (in trace amounts), and Thorium are found naturally.

8. The correct electronic configuration of Cr³⁺ (Z = 24) is:

A. [Ar] 3d³
B. [Ar] 3d⁴ 4s¹
C. [Ar] 3d² 4s¹
D. [Ar] 3d⁵ 4s¹
Answer: A

Chromium (Cr) has the electronic configuration [Ar] 3d⁵ 4s¹. Upon losing 3 electrons to form Cr³⁺, the configuration becomes [Ar] 3d³, as the 4s electrons are lost first.

9. Which of the following is a characteristic property of transition metals?

A. High electronegativity
B. Formation of colored compounds
C. Low melting and boiling points
D. Non-metallic behavior
Answer: B

Transition metals are known for forming colored compounds due to d-d transitions. They typically have low electronegativity, high melting/boiling points, and exhibit metallic behavior.

10. The actinoid contraction is similar to lanthanoid contraction but is less pronounced because:

A. 5f electrons shield more effectively than 4f electrons.
B. The nuclear charge in actinoids is lower than in lanthanoids.
C. Actinoids have larger atomic radii than lanthanoids.
D. 5f orbitals are more diffuse and shield less effectively than 4f orbitals.
Answer: D

The actinoid contraction is less pronounced because 5f orbitals are more diffuse and provide poorer shielding compared to 4f orbitals, leading to a smaller decrease in atomic radii.

11. Which of the following pairs of transition metal ions are isoelectronic?

A. Fe²⁺ and Mn²⁺
B. Cr³⁺ and V²⁺
C. Co³⁺ and Ni²⁺
D. Cu²⁺ and Zn²⁺
Answer: B

Cr³⁺ (Z = 24, electrons = 21) and V²⁺ (Z = 23, electrons = 21) both have 21 electrons, making them isoelectronic. The other pairs do not have the same number of electrons.

12. The primary reason for the catalytic activity of transition metals is:

A. Their high electronegativity
B. The presence of vacant d-orbitals
C. Their low melting points
D. Their non-metallic nature
Answer: B

Transition metals exhibit catalytic activity due to the presence of vacant d-orbitals, which allow them to form intermediate complexes with reactants, lowering the activation energy of reactions.

13. The magnetic moment of a transition metal ion is 3.87 BM. The number of unpaired electrons in the ion is:

A. 2
B. 3
C. 4
D. 5
Answer: B

The magnetic moment (μ) is related to the number of unpaired electrons (n) by the formula: μ = √[n(n+2)] BM. Given μ = 3.87 BM, solving for n: 3.87 = √[n(n+2)] → 14.98 ≈ n(n+2). Solving the quadratic equation, n ≈ 3. Thus, the ion has 3 unpaired electrons.

14. Which of the following actinides is NOT found naturally in significant quantities?

A. Thorium
B. Uranium
C. Plutonium
D. Actinium
Answer: C

Thorium, uranium, and actinium are naturally occurring actinides found in the Earth's crust. Plutonium, however, is primarily synthetic and is produced artificially in nuclear reactors, with only trace amounts found naturally.

15. Which of the following complexes exhibits a coordination number of 8?

A. [Co(NH₃)₆]³⁺
B. [Fe(CN)₆]⁴⁻
C. [La(EDTA)]⁻
D. [UO₂]²⁺
Answer: C

The complex [La(EDTA)]⁻ exhibits a coordination number of 8 because EDTA is a hexadentate ligand, and lanthanum (La) can accommodate additional coordination due to its large size. The other complexes have coordination numbers of 6.

16. Which of the following statements about the f-block elements is correct?

A. All lanthanoids exhibit a +3 oxidation state exclusively.
B. Actinoids are more reactive than lanthanoids.
C. Lanthanoids are primarily used in nuclear reactors due to their radioactivity.
D. The ionic radii of actinoids decrease more sharply than those of lanthanoids.
Answer: B

Actinoids are generally more reactive than lanthanoids due to their larger atomic sizes and lower ionization energies. While lanthanoids commonly exhibit a +3 oxidation state, some also show +2 or +4 states. Lanthanoids are not primarily radioactive, and the ionic radii of actinoids decrease less sharply than those of lanthanoids due to poorer shielding by 5f electrons.

17. The correct IUPAC name for the complex [Co(NH₃)₄Cl₂]⁺ is:

A. Tetraamminedichlorocobalt(III) ion
B. Tetraamminedichlorocobaltate(III) ion
C. Diamminetetrachlorocobalt(III) ion
D. Dichlorotetraamminecobalt(III) ion
Answer: D

The IUPAC nomenclature for coordination compounds lists ligands in alphabetical order, followed by the metal name with its oxidation state in Roman numerals. Here, the ligands are ammonia (ammine) and chloride (chloro), and the metal is cobalt in the +3 oxidation state. The correct name is dichlorotetraamminecobalt(III) ion.

18. Which of the following transition metals forms a stable carbonyl compound with the formula M(CO)₆?

A. Iron
B. Chromium
C. Nickel
D. Copper
Answer: B

Chromium forms a stable hexacarbonyl compound, Cr(CO)₆, where chromium is in the zero oxidation state. Iron forms Fe(CO)₅, nickel forms Ni(CO)₄, and copper does not form a stable carbonyl compound.

19. The phenomenon of 'lanthanoid contraction' is responsible for the similarity in properties between which of the following pairs of elements?

A. Zr and Hf
B. Nb and Ta
C. Mo and W
D. All of the above
Answer: D

Lanthanoid contraction causes the atomic radii of second and third transition series elements to be nearly identical. This results in striking similarities in properties between pairs such as Zr and Hf, Nb and Ta, and Mo and W. Thus, all the given pairs are correct.

20. Which of the following actinoids is used as a fuel in nuclear reactors?

A. Thorium
B. Plutonium
C. Uranium
D. Both B and C
Answer: D

Both uranium (U-235) and plutonium (Pu-239) are used as fuels in nuclear reactors. Thorium (Th-232) is also a potential nuclear fuel but is not as commonly used as uranium and plutonium.

21. The correct order of increasing ionic radii for the following ions is:

A. Ti⁴⁺ < Sc³⁺ < Ca²⁺ < K⁺
B. Sc³⁺ < Ti⁴⁺ < Ca²⁺ < K⁺
C. Ti⁴⁺ < Ca²⁺ < Sc³⁺ < K⁺
D. Ca²⁺ < Sc³⁺ < Ti⁴⁺ < K⁺
Answer: A

Ionic radii decrease with increasing nuclear charge for ions with the same electronic configuration. Ti⁴⁺ (3d⁰) has the smallest radius due to its highest charge, followed by Sc³⁺ (3d⁰), Ca²⁺ (3p⁶), and K⁺ (3p⁶), which has the largest radius due to its lowest charge.

22. Which of the following statements about the catalytic properties of transition metals is correct?

A. Transition metals are poor catalysts due to their high ionization energies.
B. The catalytic activity of transition metals is primarily due to their ability to exhibit variable oxidation states.
C. Transition metals cannot form intermediate complexes with reactants.
D. The catalytic activity of transition metals decreases with an increase in the number of unpaired electrons.
Answer: B

Transition metals are excellent catalysts due to their ability to exhibit variable oxidation states, which allows them to form intermediate complexes with reactants. This facilitates the reaction pathway and lowers the activation energy, enhancing catalytic activity.
