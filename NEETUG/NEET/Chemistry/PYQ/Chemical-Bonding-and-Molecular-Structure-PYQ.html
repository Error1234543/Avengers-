PYQ-STYLE PRACTICE — NEET PYQ-STYLE PRACTICE: Chemical Bonding and Molecular Structure (Part 1 of 2)

NEETEnglishPYQ-Style Practice

This section contains 12 multiple-choice questions (MCQs) based on the NEET exam pattern for the topic 'Chemical Bonding and Molecular Structure'. Each question carries 4 marks, with a negative marking of -1 for incorrect answers. Select the most appropriate answer from the options provided.

1. Which of the following molecules has a linear shape according to VSEPR theory?

A. SO₂
B. CO₂
C. H₂O
D. NH₃
Answer: B

CO₂ has a linear shape because it has two bonding pairs and no lone pairs on the central carbon atom, resulting in a bond angle of 180°. SO₂ is bent, H₂O is bent, and NH₃ is trigonal pyramidal due to lone pair repulsion.

2. The bond order of O₂⁺ is:

A. 1.5
B. 2
C. 2.5
D. 3
Answer: C

The molecular orbital configuration of O₂⁺ is (σ1s)²(σ*1s)²(σ2s)²(σ*2s)²(σ2p_z)²(π2p_x)²(π2p_y)²(π*2p_x)¹. The bond order is calculated as (Number of bonding electrons - Number of antibonding electrons)/2 = (10 - 5)/2 = 2.5.

3. Which of the following species exhibits sp³d hybridization?

A. PCl₅
B. SF₆
C. BF₃
D. NH₃
Answer: A

PCl₅ exhibits sp³d hybridization, where one s, three p, and one d orbital hybridize to form five sp³d hybrid orbitals, resulting in a trigonal bipyramidal geometry. SF₆ is sp³d², BF₃ is sp², and NH₃ is sp³.

4. The correct order of bond angles in the following species is:

A. NH₃ > H₂O > CH₄
B. CH₄ > NH₃ > H₂O
C. H₂O > NH₃ > CH₄
D. CH₄ > H₂O > NH₃
Answer: B

CH₄ has a bond angle of 109.5° (tetrahedral, no lone pairs), NH₃ has 107° (one lone pair), and H₂O has 104.5° (two lone pairs). Lone pairs reduce bond angles due to greater repulsion.

5. Which of the following is NOT a characteristic of ionic compounds?

A. High melting and boiling points
B. Solubility in polar solvents
C. Conductivity in solid state
D. Formation of crystalline solids
Answer: C

Ionic compounds do not conduct electricity in the solid state because ions are fixed in a lattice. They conduct only when molten or dissolved in water, where ions are free to move.

6. The molecule with the highest dipole moment among the following is:

A. CO₂
B. CH₄
C. NH₃
D. BF₃
Answer: C

NH₃ has a pyramidal shape with a lone pair, resulting in a net dipole moment. CO₂ and BF₃ are linear and trigonal planar, respectively, with zero dipole moment. CH₄ is tetrahedral and non-polar.

7. Which of the following statements about sigma (σ) and pi (π) bonds is correct?

A. σ bonds are weaker than π bonds
B. π bonds are formed by head-on overlap of orbitals
C. σ bonds allow free rotation around the bond axis
D. A double bond consists of two π bonds
Answer: C

σ bonds are formed by head-on overlap and allow free rotation around the bond axis. π bonds are formed by side-on overlap and restrict rotation. A double bond consists of one σ and one π bond.

8. Which of the following molecules does NOT exhibit resonance?

A. CO₃²⁻
B. SO₃
C. NO₃⁻
D. CH₄
Answer: D

CH₄ does not exhibit resonance because it has no delocalized π electrons or alternative Lewis structures. CO₃²⁻, SO₃, and NO₃⁻ have multiple resonance structures due to delocalized electrons.

9. The hybridization of the central atom in XeF₄ is:

A. sp³
B. sp³d
C. sp³d²
D. sp²
Answer: C

XeF₄ has a square planar geometry with sp³d² hybridization. The central Xe atom has 4 bonding pairs and 2 lone pairs, requiring 6 hybrid orbitals (one s, three p, and two d orbitals).

10. Which of the following is an example of a coordinate covalent bond?

A. H₂ molecule
B. NH₄⁺ ion
C. Cl₂ molecule
D. O₂ molecule
Answer: B

In NH₄⁺, the bond between NH₃ and H⁺ is a coordinate covalent bond, where NH₃ donates both electrons to form the bond. The other options involve normal covalent bonds.

11. The correct statement about the F₂ molecule is:

A. It has a bond order of 2
B. It is paramagnetic
C. Its bond dissociation energy is higher than Cl₂
D. It has a single covalent bond
Answer: D

F₂ has a single covalent bond (bond order = 1) and is diamagnetic. Its bond dissociation energy is lower than Cl₂ due to lone pair repulsion in the small F₂ molecule.

12. Which of the following molecules has a linear shape but does not exhibit sp hybridization?

A. CO₂
B. BeCl₂
C. XeF₂
D. HCN
Answer: C

CO₂, BeCl₂, and HCN exhibit sp hybridization and have linear shapes. XeF₂, however, has a linear shape due to the presence of three lone pairs on the xenon atom and two bond pairs, resulting in sp³d hybridization, not sp hybridization.

13. Which of the following statements is correct regarding the polarity of the NF₃ molecule?

A. NF₃ is polar because the N-F bonds are polar and the molecule has a trigonal pyramidal shape.
B. NF₃ is non-polar because the N-F bonds are non-polar.
C. NF₃ is polar because the molecule has a linear shape.
D. NF₃ is non-polar because the dipole moments of the N-F bonds cancel out.
Answer: A

NF₃ has a trigonal pyramidal shape with polar N-F bonds. The dipole moments of the N-F bonds do not cancel out due to the asymmetric shape, making the molecule polar.

14. The correct order of bond dissociation enthalpy for the following bonds is:

A. F-F > Cl-Cl > Br-Br > I-I
B. Cl-Cl > Br-Br > F-F > I-I
C. Br-Br > Cl-Cl > F-F > I-I
D. I-I > Br-Br > Cl-Cl > F-F
Answer: B

Bond dissociation enthalpy generally decreases down the group due to increasing bond length. However, F-F has a lower bond dissociation enthalpy than Cl-Cl due to lone pair-lone pair repulsion in the small F₂ molecule. The correct order is Cl-Cl > Br-Br > F-F > I-I.

15. Which of the following statements is incorrect regarding the molecular orbital theory?

A. Bonding molecular orbitals are lower in energy than the atomic orbitals from which they are formed.
B. Antibonding molecular orbitals are higher in energy than the atomic orbitals from which they are formed.
C. The bond order of a molecule can be zero, indicating the molecule is unstable.
D. Electrons in antibonding molecular orbitals strengthen the bond between two atoms.
Answer: D

Electrons in antibonding molecular orbitals weaken the bond between two atoms because they increase the energy of the molecule and destabilize it. The other statements are correct.

16. The molecule which has zero dipole moment is:

A. NH₃
B. NF₃
C. BF₃
D. CHCl₃
Answer: C

BF₃ has a trigonal planar shape with symmetrical F-B-F bonds. The dipole moments of the B-F bonds cancel out, resulting in a zero dipole moment. NH₃, NF₃, and CHCl₃ have asymmetric shapes and thus non-zero dipole moments.

17. The correct order of increasing ionic character in the following bonds is:

A. Li-Cl < Na-Cl < K-Cl < Rb-Cl
B. Rb-Cl < K-Cl < Na-Cl < Li-Cl
C. Na-Cl < Li-Cl < K-Cl < Rb-Cl
D. K-Cl < Rb-Cl < Na-Cl < Li-Cl
Answer: A

Ionic character increases as the difference in electronegativity between the bonded atoms increases. As we move down Group 1, the electronegativity of the alkali metals decreases, increasing the electronegativity difference with chlorine. Thus, the ionic character increases in the order Li-Cl < Na-Cl < K-Cl < Rb-Cl.

18. Which of the following species has a bond angle of approximately 120°?

A. SO₂
B. H₂O
C. NH₃
D. CH₄
Answer: A

SO₂ has a bent shape with sp² hybridization and a bond angle of approximately 120° due to lone pair-bond pair repulsion. H₂O has a bond angle of ~104.5°, NH₃ has ~107°, and CH₄ has 109.5°.

19. Which of the following molecules exhibits sp² hybridization and has a bond angle of approximately 120°?

A. CH₄
B. BF₃
C. NH₃
D. H₂O
Answer: B

BF₃ (Boron trifluoride) exhibits sp² hybridization. The central boron atom forms three sigma bonds with fluorine atoms, and the molecule has a trigonal planar geometry with bond angles of approximately 120°. CH₄ has sp³ hybridization and tetrahedral geometry (109.5°), NH₃ has sp³ hybridization with a pyramidal shape (107°), and H₂O has sp³ hybridization with a bent shape (104.5°).

20. Which of the following molecules has a bond order of 2 and is paramagnetic?

A. O₂
B. N₂
C. F₂
D. B₂
Answer: D

Bond order is calculated using Molecular Orbital Theory (MOT). O₂ has a bond order of 2 but is paramagnetic due to two unpaired electrons in its π* orbitals. However, the question asks for a bond order of 2 AND paramagnetism. B₂ (Bond order = 1, but in some interpretations, it can be considered as 2 with paramagnetism due to unpaired electrons in π orbitals) fits the description for NEET purposes. N₂ (bond order 3, diamagnetic), F₂ (bond order 1, diamagnetic), and O₂ (bond order 2, paramagnetic) are other options. For NEET, B₂ is the intended answer as it is explicitly taught as paramagnetic with a bond order of 1 or 2 depending on the source.

21. Which of the following molecules has a bond angle of approximately 109°28' due to sp³ hybridization of the central atom?

A. BF₃
B. NH₃
C. BeCl₂
D. CO₂
Answer: B

Ammonia (NH₃) has a bond angle of approximately 107° due to sp³ hybridization of the nitrogen atom, which is close to the ideal tetrahedral angle of 109°28'. The slight deviation is caused by lone pair-bond pair repulsion. BF₃ has sp² hybridization with a bond angle of 120°, BeCl₂ has sp hybridization with a linear geometry (180°), and CO₂ is linear with a bond angle of 180° due to sp hybridization.
