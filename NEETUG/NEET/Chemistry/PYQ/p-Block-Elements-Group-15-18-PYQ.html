This set contains 12 exam-focused multiple-choice questions (MCQs) designed to mimic the style, difficulty, and coverage of actual NEET previous-year questions (PYQ) for the topic 'p-Block Elements (Group 15-18)'. Each question carries +4 marks for a correct answer and -1 mark for an incorrect answer. Select the most appropriate option for each question.

1. Which of the following elements of Group 15 exhibits the most pronounced metallic character?

A. Nitrogen
B. Phosphorus
C. Arsenic
D. Bismuth
Answer: D

In Group 15, metallic character increases down the group due to the decrease in ionization enthalpy and increase in atomic size. Bismuth, being the heaviest element in the group, exhibits the most pronounced metallic character, forming compounds like BiCl3 and Bi2O3 that show metallic properties.

2. The correct order of bond angles in the hydrides of Group 15 elements is:

A. NH3 > PH3 > AsH3 > SbH3
B. PH3 > NH3 > AsH3 > SbH3
C. NH3 > AsH3 > PH3 > SbH3
D. SbH3 > AsH3 > PH3 > NH3
Answer: A

The bond angle in the hydrides of Group 15 elements decreases down the group due to the decrease in electronegativity and increase in atomic size. NH3 has the highest bond angle (107°) due to strong lone pair-bond pair repulsion, followed by PH3 (93.5°), AsH3 (91.8°), and SbH3 (91.3°).

3. Which of the following oxides of nitrogen is paramagnetic?

A. N2O
B. NO
C. N2O3
D. N2O5
Answer: B

Nitric oxide (NO) is paramagnetic because it has an odd number of electrons (11 valence electrons), resulting in an unpaired electron. This makes it attracted to magnetic fields. The other oxides listed (N2O, N2O3, N2O5) are diamagnetic as they have all electrons paired.

4. The correct statement regarding the basicity of hydrides of Group 15 elements is:

A. NH3 is less basic than PH3 due to higher electronegativity of nitrogen.
B. Basicity increases from NH3 to BiH3 due to increasing atomic size.
C. NH3 is the most basic due to the highest electron density on nitrogen.
D. PH3 is more basic than NH3 because phosphorus is less electronegative.
Answer: C

The basicity of Group 15 hydrides decreases down the group due to the decrease in electron density on the central atom. NH3 is the most basic because nitrogen, being the most electronegative, holds the lone pair of electrons more tightly, making it readily available for donation. PH3 and other hydrides are much less basic.

5. Which of the following is NOT a property of white phosphorus?

A. It is highly reactive and spontaneously ignites in air.
B. It consists of P4 tetrahedral molecules.
C. It is insoluble in carbon disulfide.
D. It glows in the dark due to slow oxidation.
Answer: C

White phosphorus is highly soluble in carbon disulfide (CS2) due to its molecular structure and nonpolar nature. It is highly reactive, consists of P4 tetrahedral molecules, spontaneously ignites in air (pyrophoric), and exhibits chemiluminescence (glows in the dark).

6. The correct order of thermal stability of the hydrides of Group 16 elements is:

A. H2O > H2S > H2Se > H2Te
B. H2Te > H2Se > H2S > H2O
C. H2S > H2O > H2Se > H2Te
D. H2O > H2Te > H2Se > H2S
Answer: A

The thermal stability of Group 16 hydrides decreases down the group due to the weakening of the E-H bond (where E is the Group 16 element). H2O is the most stable due to strong hydrogen bonding and high bond dissociation energy, followed by H2S, H2Se, and H2Te.

7. The correct order of acid strength for the oxyacids of chlorine is:

A. HClO < HClO2 < HClO3 < HClO4
B. HClO4 < HClO3 < HClO2 < HClO
C. HClO2 < HClO < HClO3 < HClO4
D. HClO3 < HClO4 < HClO2 < HClO
Answer: A

The acid strength of the oxyacids of chlorine increases with the oxidation state of chlorine. HClO4 (perchloric acid) is the strongest, followed by HClO3 (chloric acid), HClO2 (chlorous acid), and HClO (hypochlorous acid). This is due to the increasing stability of the conjugate base as the number of oxygen atoms increases.

8. Which of the following noble gases is NOT used in gas discharge tubes to produce colored light?

A. Helium
B. Neon
C. Argon
D. Radon
Answer: D

Radon is radioactive and not used in gas discharge tubes due to its health hazards. Helium (yellow light), Neon (red-orange light), and Argon (blue or purple light) are commonly used in gas discharge tubes for producing colored light.

9. The correct statement about the interhalogen compound ICl3 is:

A. It is a linear molecule with sp hybridization.
B. It is a T-shaped molecule with sp3d hybridization.
C. It is a square planar molecule with dsp2 hybridization.
D. It is a tetrahedral molecule with sp3 hybridization.
Answer: B

ICl3 is a T-shaped molecule due to the presence of 5 electron pairs (3 bonding pairs and 2 lone pairs) around the central iodine atom. This results in sp3d hybridization. The bond angles are less than 90° due to lone pair repulsion.

10. Which of the following is the correct reason for the anomalous behavior of fluorine in Group 17?

A. High electronegativity and small atomic size.
B. Low bond dissociation energy of F-F bond.
C. Absence of d-orbitals in the valence shell.
D. All of the above.
Answer: D

Fluorine exhibits anomalous behavior in Group 17 due to its high electronegativity, small atomic size, low F-F bond dissociation energy (due to lone pair repulsion), and the absence of d-orbitals in its valence shell, which limits its oxidation states and coordination number.

11. The correct order of boiling points of the hydrides of Group 16 elements is:

A. H2O > H2Te > H2Se > H2S
B. H2O > H2S > H2Se > H2Te
C. H2Te > H2Se > H2S > H2O
D. H2S > H2Se > H2Te > H2O
Answer: A

The boiling points of Group 16 hydrides are influenced by hydrogen bonding. H2O has the highest boiling point due to strong hydrogen bonding. Among the others, boiling points increase with molecular weight: H2Te > H2Se > H2S. Thus, the correct order is H2O > H2Te > H2Se > H2S.

12. Which of the following statements about the allotropy of phosphorus is correct?

A. White phosphorus is more stable than red phosphorus at room temperature.
B. Black phosphorus is the most reactive allotrope of phosphorus.
C. Red phosphorus is polymeric and less reactive than white phosphorus.
D. White phosphorus exists as discrete P2 molecules in the solid state.
Answer: C

Red phosphorus is polymeric in nature, making it less reactive and more stable than white phosphorus, which exists as discrete P4 molecules and is highly reactive. Black phosphorus is the most stable and least reactive allotrope.

13. Which of the following noble gases is used in the treatment of cancer?

A. Helium
B. Neon
C. Argon
D. Radon
Answer: D

Radon is radioactive and is used in radiotherapy for the treatment of cancer. The other noble gases listed are not radioactive and do not have this application.

14. The correct statement about the structure of P4O10 is:

A. It has a tetrahedral structure with phosphorus at the center.
B. It consists of discrete P4O10 molecules with a cage-like structure.
C. It is a linear molecule with alternating P and O atoms.
D. It forms a polymeric chain structure in the solid state.
Answer: B

P4O10 has a cage-like structure where four phosphorus atoms are arranged tetrahedrally, and each phosphorus atom is bonded to oxygen atoms, forming a discrete molecular structure.

15. Which of the following is NOT a property of interhalogen compounds?

A. They are more reactive than the individual halogens.
B. They are all diamagnetic in nature.
C. They can act as both oxidizing and fluorinating agents.
D. They are formed by the combination of two different halogens.
Answer: B

Interhalogen compounds are paramagnetic if they contain an odd number of electrons (e.g., ClF3). They are more reactive than individual halogens and can act as oxidizing and fluorinating agents.

16. Which of the following statements about the Haber process is correct?

A. It involves the direct combination of nitrogen and hydrogen to form ammonia at low pressure.
B. It uses a catalyst of finely divided iron with promoters like Al2O3 and K2O.
C. It is an endothermic reaction, requiring high temperatures for optimal yield.
D. It produces ammonia in a single step without any equilibrium considerations.
Answer: B

The Haber process uses a catalyst of finely divided iron with promoters like Al2O3 and K2O to increase the rate of reaction. It is an exothermic reaction and is carried out at high pressure to favor ammonia formation.

17. The correct order of bond dissociation enthalpy for the halogens is:

A. F2 > Cl2 > Br2 > I2
B. Cl2 > Br2 > F2 > I2
C. I2 > Br2 > Cl2 > F2
D. Br2 > Cl2 > F2 > I2
Answer: B

The bond dissociation enthalpy decreases down the group due to increasing atomic size and weaker bonds. The order is Cl2 > Br2 > F2 > I2 (F2 has a lower bond dissociation enthalpy due to lone pair repulsion).

18. Which of the following is the correct formula for Marshall's acid?

A. H2S2O8
B. H2SO5
C. H2S2O7
D. H2SO4
Answer: A

Marshall's acid is peroxydisulfuric acid, with the formula H2S2O8. It is a strong oxidizing agent and contains a peroxide linkage.

19. The correct statement about the inert pair effect in Group 15 elements is:

A. It becomes more pronounced down the group, leading to the +3 oxidation state being more stable than +5 for heavier elements.
B. It causes the +5 oxidation state to be more stable than the +3 oxidation state for all elements in the group.
C. It is responsible for the high reactivity of bismuth in its +5 oxidation state.
D. It is absent in nitrogen and phosphorus but present in arsenic and antimony.
Answer: A

The inert pair effect becomes more pronounced down Group 15, making the +3 oxidation state more stable than the +5 oxidation state for heavier elements like bismuth. This is due to the reluctance of the s-electrons to participate in bonding.

20. Which of the following statements regarding the noble gases is INCORRECT?

A. Helium has the lowest boiling point among all known substances due to its weak van der Waals forces.
B. Xenon forms compounds with fluorine under specific conditions, such as XeF2, XeF4, and XeF6.
C. Argon is the most abundant noble gas in the Earth's atmosphere, constituting about 0.93% by volume.
D. Radon is chemically inert and does not form any compounds under any conditions, similar to neon.
Answer: D

Option D is incorrect because radon, unlike neon, is not entirely chemically inert. While radon is radioactive and has a short half-life, it can form compounds such as radon fluoride (RnF2) under specific conditions. The other options are correct: Helium has the lowest boiling point due to weak van der Waals forces (A), xenon forms compounds like XeF2, XeF4, and XeF6 (B), and argon is the most abundant noble gas in the atmosphere (C).

21. Which of the following statements regarding the hydrides of Group 15 elements is correct?

A. The basicity of hydrides increases down the group from NH₃ to BiH₃.
B. The thermal stability of hydrides decreases down the group due to weakening of the E-H bond (E = N, P, As, Sb, Bi).
C. Ammonia (NH₃) is the strongest reducing agent among the Group 15 hydrides.
D. The bond angle in PH₃ is greater than that in NH₃ due to larger size of phosphorus.
Answer: B

The thermal stability of Group 15 hydrides decreases down the group (NH₃ > PH₃ > AsH₃ > SbH₃ > BiH₃) due to the weakening of the E-H bond as the atomic size increases. Option A is incorrect because basicity decreases down the group (NH₃ is the most basic). Option C is incorrect as BiH₃ is the strongest reducing agent. Option D is incorrect because the bond angle in PH₃ (93.5°) is smaller than in NH₃ (107°) due to lesser s-character in the hybrid orbitals of phosphorus.

22. A compound X reacts with hot and concentrated NaOH to give a gas Y, which when passed through alkaline pyrogallol solution turns it dark brown. The compound X is most likely:

A. NH₄Cl
B. PCl₅
C. H₂SO₄
D. KNO₃
Answer: B

PCl₅ reacts with hot and concentrated NaOH to produce phosphine (PH₃) gas, which is the gas Y. PH₃ turns alkaline pyrogallol solution dark brown due to its reducing properties. NH₄Cl (Option A) produces NH₃, which does not react with pyrogallol. H₂SO₄ (Option C) and KNO₃ (Option D) do not produce PH₃ under these conditions.

23. Which of the following noble gases is used in the treatment of cancer and is also found in trace amounts in the atmosphere?

A. Helium
B. Neon
C. Argon
D. Radon
Answer: D

Radon (Rn) is a radioactive noble gas used in radiotherapy for cancer treatment. It is also found in trace amounts in the atmosphere due to the decay of radium in the Earth's crust. Helium (Option A) is used in cryogenics, Neon (Option B) in lighting, and Argon (Option C) in welding, but none are used in cancer treatment.
