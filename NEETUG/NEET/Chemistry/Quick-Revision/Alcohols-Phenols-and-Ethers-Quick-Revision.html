Definitions

General formula: R-OH, where R is an alkyl group. Classified as primary (1°), secondary (2°), or tertiary (3°) based on the carbon to which the -OH group is attached.'}
  • {'term': 'Phenols', 'content': 'Aromatic compounds where a hydroxyl (-OH) group is directly bonded to an sp² hybridized carbon of a benzene ring. General formula: Ar-OH, where Ar is an aryl group. More acidic than alcohols due to resonance stabilization of the phenoxide ion.'}
  • {'term': 'Ethers', 'content': "Organic compounds with an oxygen atom bonded to two alkyl or aryl groups. General formula: R-O-R', where R and R' can be the same or different. Classified as symmetrical (R = R') or unsymmetrical (R ≠ R')."}

Important facts

  • Methanol (CH₃OH) is highly toxic and causes blindness or death if ingested. Used as a solvent and antifreeze.
  • Ethanol (C₂H₅OH) is the only alcohol safe for human consumption (in moderation). Used in alcoholic beverages, disinfectants, and as a biofuel.
  • Phenol is corrosive and causes burns on skin contact. Used in the manufacture of plastics (Bakelite), drugs (aspirin), and disinfectants.
  • Diethyl ether (C₂H₅OC₂H₅) was historically used as an anesthetic but is now replaced due to flammability and side effects.
  • Epoxides (cyclic ethers with a three-membered ring) are highly reactive due to ring strain. Example: Ethylene oxide (used in sterilization).
  • Glycerol (1,2,3-propanetriol) is a trihydric alcohol used in cosmetics, pharmaceuticals, and as a sweetener.
  • Picric acid (2,4,6-trinitrophenol) is a highly explosive phenol derivative used in munitions.
