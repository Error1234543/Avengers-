NEET Chemistry Quick Revision: Solid State

NEETEnglishQuick Revision

Definitions

  • Solid State: The state of matter characterized by structural rigidity, fixed shape, and volume, where constituent particles (atoms, ions, or molecules) are closely packed in a regular geometric pattern.
  • Crystal Lattice: A three-dimensional arrangement of points representing the positions of particles (atoms, ions, or molecules) in a crystalline solid.
  • Unit Cell: The smallest repeating unit in a crystal lattice that, when repeated in three dimensions, generates the entire lattice.
  • Coordination Number: The number of nearest neighbor particles surrounding a central particle in a crystal lattice.
  • Packing Efficiency: The percentage of total space occupied by the constituent particles in a unit cell, calculated as (Volume occupied by particles / Total volume of unit cell) × 100.
  • Defects in Crystals: Irregularities or imperfections in the arrangement of particles in a crystal lattice, classified as point defects (vacancies, interstitial, impurities) or line defects (dislocations).
  • Electrical Conductivity in Solids: The ability of a solid to conduct electricity, determined by the presence of free or delocalized electrons (metallic conductors), ions (ionic conductors), or absence of charge carriers (insulators).

Important facts

  • The **coordination number** in a simple cubic unit cell is 6, while in BCC it is 8, and in FCC/CCP it is 12.
  • The **packing efficiency** of HCP and CCP (FCC) is the same (74%), higher than BCC (68%) and SC (52%).
  • In **ionic solids**, the radius ratio (r₊/r₋) determines the coordination number and geometry: 0.155–0.225 (triangular planar), 0.225–0.414 (tetrahedral), 0.414–0.732 (octahedral), 0.732–1.0 (cubic).
  • **Schottky defect** is a vacancy defect in ionic solids where equal numbers of cations and anions are missing, maintaining electrical neutrality (e.g., NaCl). **Frenkel defect** is an interstitial defect where a smaller ion moves to an interstitial site (e.g., AgCl).
  • The **density of a unit cell** can be calculated using ρ = (Z × M) / (a³ × Nₐ). For example, in FCC copper (a = 3.61 Å, M = 63.5 g/mol), Z = 4, so ρ = (4 × 63.5) / (3.61 × 10⁻⁸)³ × 6.022 × 10²³ ≈ 8.96 g/cm³.
  • **F-centers** (Farbenzenter) are anion vacancies occupied by electrons, responsible for color in alkali halides (e.g., purple color in KCl due to F-centers).
  • **Semiconductors** exhibit negative temperature coefficient of resistance (conductivity increases with temperature), unlike metals.
  • **Superconductors** are materials that exhibit zero electrical resistance below a critical temperature (Tₚ), e.g., YBa₂Cu₃O₇ at 92 K.

Memory aids

  • **Cubic Unit Cells**: Remember '1-2-4' for atoms per unit cell: SC (1), BCC (2), FCC (4).
